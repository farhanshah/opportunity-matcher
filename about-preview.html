01 / OUR PURPOSE

A better way to
begin the search.

Opportunity research can mean dozens of sources, different criteria and application cycles that change. OppMatcher aims to organize those details and give applicants a clearer place to begin.

Our matching signals are guidance, not a provider's admission decision. We design around transparency: if a rule cannot be confirmed, it should remain an open question, not become a confident answer.

Built around the
source of truth.

Based on our published research methodology. Programme-specific rules and current application details belong to the organization responsible for the opportunity.

01 / AUTHORITY

Official provider first.

Search engines and third-party lists can surface leads. Published eligibility, funding and application requirements should come from the official provider.

02 / TIMING

Dates need context.

Old calls can remain online. A past cycle's deadline should never be presented as a current deadline without fresh confirmation.

03 / ELIGIBILITY

No invented certainty.

Nationality, residency, degree equivalency and experience rules may depend on the individual applicant. Missing evidence requires review, not automatic approval.

03 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Answers, without
the guesswork.

How discovery, Passport matching and evidence work in OppMatcher. This is a design preview; current functionality remains in the live application.

Does OppMatcher guarantee that I qualify?

No. Matching can highlight programme fit and questions to check, but the provider sets the final criteria and makes the decision. Confirm the nationality, education and other conditions with the official source.

Where do opportunity records come from?

The platform monitors a registry of official providers and researches additional worldwide opportunities. Web-discovered leads are not automatically considered verified just because a search engine returned them.

What if a deadline or funding detail is missing?

An unclear requirement should remain flagged for a provider check. Applications can change between cycles, and an older page may not describe the current call.

What is the Opportunity Passport?

It is the applicant profile used to organize relevant matching signals, such as nationality, education, field of study and preferences. It does not replace the official application or eligibility decision.

Can I save and track my applications?

The live OppMatcher workspace includes Saved and Tracker features. You can view the proposed new design in the workspace preview.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Review the live privacy policy for details about accounts, Passport information, data handling and contact options. The legal text has not been changed as part of these previews.
