About OppMatcher

Find the right opportunity. Check the evidence.

OppMatcher is an independent online service for people comparing scholarships, internships, fellowships and other international opportunities. We combine public provider information with a personal Opportunity Passport, a matching workspace and application tracking.

Finding an opportunity is only the first decision. Applicants also need to know whether it is actually open, whether they meet the requirements and where the official application lives. OppMatcher aims to make those checks easier without treating an old programme announcement as a current vacancy.

Discover

Browse recent opportunities and provider information. Records with a specific, recently checked vacancy are distinguished from general programme announcements and unconfirmed leads.

Assess fit

Use the Opportunity Passport to organise facts about nationality, education, field and funding preferences. The tool assists with preliminary screening; it does not decide admission, visa eligibility or scholarship awards.

Prepare and track

Save your shortlist, monitor application stages and use our practical guides to prepare documents before applying directly with the official provider.

How to read our evidence labels

What you seeWhat it means
Recently verified open vacancyA specific job or internship posting passed the current exact-source checks and its recorded deadline has not passed. Verify the latest status on the official page before applying.
Source-confirmed programmeThe programme or official provider page exists, but the exact intake, deadline or your personal eligibility may still be unconfirmed.
Needs recheckThe stored evidence is too old or incomplete to call the opportunity currently open.
ClosedA recorded deadline has passed or the provider has confirmed the opening is closed. Historical pages are retained for reference, not advertised as applications currently being accepted.

A high confidence score is never a guarantee of acceptance. Individual provider announcements and official application portals take precedence over our summaries.

How we research and correct information

Our source monitoring and editorial process prioritise official programme pages and individual application notices. Some sources block automated access or publish information in formats that cannot be checked reliably. When verification fails, we should label the uncertainty rather than invent dates, funding or eligibility conditions.

Our research methodology explains the source hierarchy, changes in deadlines, eligibility restrictions and corrections. We welcome reports of broken official links, misleading labels, missing country restrictions and outdated content.
