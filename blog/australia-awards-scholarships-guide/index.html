Scholarships in Australia

Australia Awards Scholarships: Country and Course Rules

Check partner-country rules, available degrees and return-home conditions.

OppMatcher editorialPublished 23 October 2026Updated 3 November 2026 with official sources

Check partner-country rules, available degrees and return-home conditions. This guide explains a programme route and does not claim that applications are currently open.

Country eligibility is the first gate

Australia Awards Scholarships are designed for eligible partner-country applicants. Find the official information pack for your country rather than treating the general overview as proof that applicants worldwide qualify. Review priority areas and any work experience conditions.

Check course and award restrictions

The scholarship may support particular study levels and subject areas. Confirm whether a proposed university programme meets the rules, and then check the institution's own academic and language requirements. These are different decisions.

Understand what happens after study

Some Australia Awards conditions involve returning home and contributing to development priorities. Read the obligations before writing an application plan. Consider whether they align with your professional goals and commitments.

Check each benefit and timing

Compare what the scholarship covers with likely living and arrival costs. Funding descriptions should be read from the current award information, including how travel and initial payments work. Never rely on the phrase fully funded to estimate all personal expenses.

Before submitting your application

Make a copy of the current official call, record the programme name and route, and confirm the time zone of any stated deadline. Check nationality, degree level, language requirements and the documents needed for your particular programme. If the relevant details are missing, contact the provider rather than treating the programme as open or as a guaranteed personal match.

Start with your country profile, not a general scholarship list

The Australian Government's Australia Awards participating countries page identifies which countries may apply. Country profiles set additional rules on priority areas, accepted degrees and documentation. A nationality being listed does not establish eligibility for every programme or course. If your country is absent from the participating-country list, the official FAQ says you cannot apply to Australia Awards Scholarships through this programme.

2027 study intake dates are already past

For most listed countries, the official 2027 intake timetable opened on 1 February 2026 and closed on 30 April 2026 at the applicable Australian time. As of 3 November 2026, this round must not be advertised as a current open call. Some country-specific arrangements can differ, so always follow that country's official timetable and avoid extrapolating a future deadline.

The official FAQ also explains that applications are not assessed on a rolling first-come-first-served basis. Submitting weeks before the deadline does not create a selection advantage; it simply helps avoid technical problems and allows time to correct an unfinished draft.

What Australia Awards generally funds

According to Australia's official scholarship information, benefits generally include tuition, one return economy airfare, a one-time establishment contribution, fortnightly living support and basic Overseas Student Health Cover for the scholar. Eligible students may receive introductory academic preparation or other programme-specific support.

Do not assume that a spouse's health insurance, full family living costs, private accommodation deposit or every flight expense is automatically covered. Read the policy handbook and your offer before planning a relocation.

Return-home requirement and its consequences

Australia Awards recipients normally must leave Australia for at least two years after completing their scholarship. The Australian Government says failure to meet this condition can create a debt for the total accrued scholarship cost. This obligation can materially affect someone planning immediate post-study migration or employment in Australia.

For example, compare two applicants who both want a public-policy master's: one intends to return to their home country to implement a project, while the other intends to remain in Australia after graduation. The return-home condition may make the second person's plan incompatible with the award, even if their academic qualifications otherwise fit.

How to apply, including the programme's AI-writing restriction

  1. Identify the country's current official application route. Most countries use OASIS, but Indonesia and the Philippines use country-specific portals.
  2. Read the eligibility rules, current country profile and policy handbook before preparing a course plan.
  3. Gather the required qualifications, evidence of work experience, references and language documents for that country.
  4. Prepare your own original response to the provider's questions, including practical development goals and a realistic post-study plan.
  5. Review the form and save evidence of submission. The official guidance warns that a submitted application cannot be edited.

AI-writing warning: The Australian Government's 2026 application instructions allow AI for basic spelling, grammar and organising ideas, but say AI must not write, improve or paraphrase any part of the application response. Do not use an automated essay generator for this scholarship. A planning checklist can help you organise deadlines and verified requirements, but the submitted application must comply with DFAT's writing rules.
