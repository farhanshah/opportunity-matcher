Chevening Interview Preparation 2027: Practice Topics

Prepare evidence, not memorised answers

What the application asks you to show

Chevening selections assess applicants against the programme's current official criteria. The questions below are practice prompts written for preparation, not leaked or confirmed interview questions. Their wording should never be represented as the exact questions an interviewer will ask.

How to structure the evidence

Practice explaining why each chosen UK course is appropriate, how prior work or study prepares you, and how you would use the degree after returning to your career. Tie claims to the course content, applicant's real record and the published scholarship conditions. Avoid promising outcomes that are beyond your control.

An example to adapt truthfully

Practice describing a difficult decision you led, a professional connection you maintained and an experience that challenged your assumptions. Focus on your personal actions, collaboration and what changed. If a result was mixed, demonstrate judgement instead of inventing success.

Before you submit

Review the official Chevening interview and selection guidance, your submitted essays and your application timeline. Prepare questions about your choices and evidence. Keep examples consistent across CV, references and statements, and be ready to say where you do not yet know a fact.

  1. Reopen the actual provider's current application requirements.
  2. Check nationality, academic stage and work permission where applicable.
  3. Use only verifiable examples and follow the document format.
