Chevening leadership essay guidance

Evidence for decisions and outcomes

How to approach it

Select a real situation where you made a meaningful decision, worked with others and can explain the result. An ordinary role title alone does not demonstrate leadership.

A useful structure covers the challenge, your action, the collaboration involved, the outcome and your reflection. Separate your individual contributions from achievements of the wider team.

Consult the current Chevening essay prompts and word limits, which may change across intakes. Check the main Chevening guide before drafting application-specific language.

Practical checklist

  1. Open the named official provider's current application instructions.
  2. Confirm nationality, residence, degree and other hard rules.
  3. Record supporting documents, time zone and submission process.

Use the free readiness checklist, compare related topic guides, and read how OppMatcher handles unverified details.

A guide is a research aid, not evidence of an open application or your eligibility. The provider's current documents control the final decision.
