Chevening Scholarships: Application and Course Planning

Plan the course shortlist, application evidence and country-specific requirements for Chevening.

OppMatcher editorialPublished 23 October 2026Updated 3 November 2026 with official sources

Plan the course shortlist, application evidence and country-specific requirements for Chevening. This guide explains a programme route and does not claim that applications are currently open.

Verify your country route first

Start with the official Chevening guidance for your country and intake. Country eligibility, professional background and post-study obligations need careful reading. A former recipient's advice can help with preparation but cannot replace the current programme requirements.

Shortlist qualifying UK courses

The university's entry rules and the scholarship's course rules are separate. Compare degree content, academic prerequisites and start dates before using a course in your application. Keep both university admissions deadlines and Chevening milestones visible in your tracker.

Describe decisions and evidence

When discussing leadership or influence, choose a genuine situation, explain your contribution and name the result. A short example from employment, volunteering or student leadership is more convincing than adjectives such as ambitious or innovative. Write about your own decisions rather than taking credit for a whole team.

Prepare across application stages

The official timeline covers stages beyond the initial submission. Track references, interview preparation, course offers and documents independently. If a page still displays last year's dates, check the live current-cycle guidance before arranging funding or travel.

Before submitting your application

Make a copy of the current official call, record the programme name and route, and confirm the time zone of any stated deadline. Check nationality, degree level, language requirements and the documents needed for your particular programme. If the relevant details are missing, contact the provider rather than treating the programme as open or as a guaranteed personal match.

Chevening eligibility: the checks that can rule you out

The official Chevening eligibility rules are more specific than a general scholarship checklist. Applicants must be citizens of eligible countries or territories and intend to return to their country of award for at least two years after the scholarship. Separate residence restrictions apply, including a restriction on people residing in the UK at the time of application, subject to the programme's stated exceptions.

Chevening requires 2,800 hours of work experience completed after finishing your undergraduate degree, and the undergraduate studies must have been completed at least two years before the application deadline. A qualifying university degree must also meet the entry requirements for a UK master's course. Do not assume that work completed before graduation counts or that a postgraduate qualification waives the undergraduate timing requirement.

Worked example: counting 2,800 hours

Suppose an applicant has 80 qualifying working weeks after graduation at an average of 35 hours per week. That is 80 × 35 = 2,800 hours. This is an arithmetic illustration, not a ruling on whether a particular job or activity qualifies. Make a separate record for each experience period, remove overlapping hours and check Chevening's published work-experience rules before entering a total in the application.

Choose three eligible courses, then apply to the universities separately

Chevening's course rules require three different UK master's courses. They may be at the same university or different institutions, but each course needs to meet Chevening's criteria. Eligible courses are UK-based, full-time, taught master's programmes starting in autumn, generally running nine to twelve months. Part-time, distance-learning and research-focused MRes courses are not accepted under these rules.

Applying to Chevening is not the same as applying to a university. Apply separately to your chosen institutions, and secure an unconditional offer for at least one of the nominated courses by the official timeline deadline. A course title on a search portal is not proof of eligibility; check course duration, location, teaching mode and current university entry criteria.

Do not mistake the last application round for an open call

Chevening's official timeline states that the scholarship application round opened in August 2026 and closed in October 2026. If you are researching after that window, prepare your evidence for the next round but do not describe the closed application as currently open. Future deadlines are not confirmed by last year's timetable. Check the application timeline and your country page for the current round.
