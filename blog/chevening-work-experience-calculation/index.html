Chevening Work Experience: How to Check and Document It

Check current work-experience rules and build a truthful record of employment and voluntary activity.

OppMatcher editorial · Evidence-first applications23 October 2026
Editorial reviewThis shorter guide is being reviewed for more detailed evidence. For updated, source-backed information, read our related programme guide and confirm any application decision at the official provider. Do not treat an older deadline or eligibility summary here as a current open call.

Check current work-experience rules and build a truthful record of employment and voluntary activity. This is an evergreen decision guide. Always confirm the latest application status, scheme conditions and closing time on the official page.

Use the present intake guidance

Scholarship eligibility rules can change. Rather than relying on an older online calculator or a past recipient's story, read the current Chevening eligibility and application instructions for your country. Check which kinds of work the scheme recognises.

Build a chronological work history

Record organisation, role, dates, hours and responsibilities for each period. Identify any overlaps so you do not count the same working hour twice. If the provider asks for minimum hours or a defined period, use the actual current method and confirm any rounding rules.

Separate evidence from job titles

A part-time support role can involve genuine initiative, while a senior-sounding title can conceal limited involvement. Describe the work you actually performed, the people you worked with and results you can support. Avoid using invented figures or inflating volunteer hours.

Align stories across forms

Use the same dates and job details in your CV and scholarship application. Prepare contact information for reference checks when requested. For career essays, explain how professional experiences connect to your intended study and realistic next steps.

Practical example

Example: a candidate combined part-time paid work with student-society volunteering. They should document each role's hours and dates and check the current scheme's eligibility rules before calculating what counts.

What to check

  1. Read the current experience policy
  2. Check overlapping employment periods
  3. Keep a consistent CV and timeline
