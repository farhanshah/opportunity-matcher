DAAD Scholarships in Germany: Find the Right Call

How to navigate the DAAD database and distinguish master's, doctoral and research funding.

OppMatcher editorialPublished 23 October 2026Updated 3 November 2026 with official-source verification

How to navigate the DAAD database and distinguish master's, doctoral and research funding. This guide explains a programme route and does not claim that applications are currently open.

Search by your real profile

DAAD offers a database of different scholarship programmes. Filter by your country, academic stage, area of study and purpose of visit. A master's course, a research stay and a doctorate are different funding situations. Save the specific award page before preparing documents.

Read the conditions of the exact scheme

Check graduation timing, professional experience, language requirements and field restrictions. An award may require a university admission, a supervisor or a research proposal. Do not assume that seeing your country in the general database establishes eligibility for every listing.

Calculate the actual funding package

DAAD support can include a monthly amount, travel, insurance or other benefits, but the combination varies by call. Use the official award description to check the payment amount and timing. Budget independently for deposits, translations or other initial expenses.

Write a course-specific application

Explain why the German programme fits your research or professional direction and give evidence of previous preparation. A focused application should identify a realistic outcome and any supervisor arrangements. Check the programme portal linked by DAAD and save the submission confirmation.

Before submitting your application

Make a copy of the current official call, record the programme name and route, and confirm the time zone of any stated deadline. Check nationality, degree level, language requirements and the documents needed for your particular programme. If the relevant details are missing, contact the provider rather than treating the programme as open or as a guaranteed personal match.

DAAD does not have one universal scholarship application

DAAD funds different types of study and research. According to DAAD's scholarship overview, opportunities include master's study scholarships, research grants for doctoral applicants, selected subject-specific courses and programmes supporting applicants studying or researching in their region. That means a rule for one scholarship cannot automatically be applied to every other DAAD award.

Use the DAAD scholarship database to search for the exact award, then open that call's application and selection tabs. The database is the route to official application instructions, rather than a generic “Apply to DAAD” form.

DAAD scholarship and university admission are separate checks

A university's language and academic admission requirements are not always identical to the scholarship's rules. DAAD's study requirements guidance explains that many German-taught courses expect German-language evidence, while English-taught programmes require the institution's accepted English proof. Some universities use uni-assist, and others handle admission directly. Check the current admissions page for the specific course.

For example, an English-taught master's applicant might be able to provide English proficiency without a German-language entrance certificate. But this does not mean every DAAD scholarship accepts the same test or exempts all language requirements. A DAAD master's scholarship entry explains how applicants may need evidence of the study language in the DAAD application and later for university admission. Use the exact entry for the current call.

Funding example: don't treat a headline amount as your budget

DAAD's overview currently gives illustrative monthly amounts of €992 for students and €1,300 for doctoral/PhD students, alongside potential travel and insurance support. These are examples from DAAD's general guidance, not guaranteed payment for every scholarship or proof of the next award cycle's amounts. Other funding, course fees, extra insurance and family support vary by programme.

An application workflow that avoids duplicate work

  1. Search the DAAD programme overview and database by your nationality, study level and field.
  2. Save the full title and link of the chosen award. Check the current deadline, award period, required experience and any country restrictions.
  3. Read the German university's programme page and confirm that its course can accept your qualification and language evidence.
  4. Make separate lists of scholarship documents and university admission documents, including any research proposal, references or supervisor contact.
  5. Check who receives the application: the DAAD portal, a university, or another body named in the exact call.
  6. Keep proof of submission and avoid claiming that merely finding the DAAD programme guarantees a place or award.

DAAD EPOS 2027 versus other DAAD scholarship routes

DAAD EPOS is linked to development-related postgraduate courses. The applicable course and DAAD selection process differ from other DAAD scholarships, and the applicant may need to apply directly to a participating course before being recommended for DAAD funding.

Read the DAAD EPOS programme explanation and the Germany scholarship hub. Confirm eligible countries, professional experience, language requirements and each course's current submission instructions.
