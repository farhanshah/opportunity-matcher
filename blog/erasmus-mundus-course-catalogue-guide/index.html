Erasmus Mundus Course Catalogue: How to Shortlist Joint Master's Degrees

Select actual Erasmus Mundus courses before assessing scholarships, mobility and deadlines.

OppMatcher editorial · Evidence-first applicationsUpdated 16 November 2026
Quick answerThe European Commission's Erasmus Mundus course catalogue helps you discover joint master's degrees, but you apply directly to the consortium offering your chosen degree. Check the course website for its current admissions and scholarship rules. There is no single application form for every programme.

Select actual Erasmus Mundus courses before assessing scholarships, mobility and deadlines. This is an evergreen decision guide. Always confirm the latest application status, scheme conditions and closing time on the official page.

Start from the programme catalogue

Erasmus Mundus is a family of joint master's degrees run by university consortia, not a single subject or scholarship form. Identify courses matching your previous study and future goals. Open the consortium page for each one and record participating institutions and the degree award.

Check admissions before the scholarship

A programme may be academically open to candidates who are not selected for funding. Confirm academic prerequisites, language requirements, required references and the separate scholarship rules. Avoid assuming any consortium in the catalogue offers a scholarship for the intake you want.

Consider mobility and city costs

Some courses require study in two or more countries. Check the intended sequence of campuses, academic start dates and immigration steps. Housing costs and travel between universities can affect affordability even with funding, and the consortium should explain how the mobility arrangement works.

Create a useful shortlist

Compare three real course options in a table covering fit, host locations, scholarship selection and submission route. Eliminate a course if a mandatory entry condition is not met. Then request transcripts and references based on the most demanding application checklist rather than relying on one generic form.

Practical example

Example: a computer science graduate considering public policy and data programmes should compare the entry modules and expected quantitative background before choosing which Erasmus Mundus consortia are realistic.

Erasmus Mundus course catalogue for 2026–2027: where to start

The European Education and Culture Executive Agency (EACEA) catalogue is the official starting point. It has filters for keywords, field of study, university, country, course duration and credits, and year of project selection. Choose the field or subject first. Then open the course website linked in its record. A familiar course name is not enough to establish entry requirements or current funding.

The 2026–2027 search has a useful distinction: the European Commission announced on 16 July 2026 that the latest Erasmus Mundus selection included 37 new Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters and 50 Erasmus Mundus Design Measures. Those are not 87 new student scholarship applications. The 37 master's consortia were scheduled to open student applications in autumn 2026 for September 2027 starts; a Design Measures award helps institutions prepare future programmes and is not the same as an active joint master recruiting students. See the Commission's 2026 selection announcement.

Is every programme in the Erasmus Mundus catalogue fully funded?

No. EACEA explicitly says some catalogue programmes do not currently offer Erasmus Mundus scholarships because EU funding is nearing its end or they have a temporary right to use the name. You therefore need two separate checks: whether the consortium is accepting students for the intake, and whether it is accepting scholarship applications for that same intake. A programme can appear in a valid official catalogue without offering funded places this year.

When looking for funded master's study, record what the consortium says about participation costs, any monthly allowance, travel, visa and insurance provisions. Do not copy a generic Erasmus Mundus scholarship value into every course's summary. Programme-specific terms and the current year's award documents are what matter.

A realistic course shortlist for 2027

For example, a graduate interested in computer science and cybersecurity might find the official catalogue includes CYBERSURE (Cybersecurity and Assurance), EDISS (Engineering of Data-intensive Intelligent Software Systems), and DEAI (Data Engineering and Artificial Intelligence). Those are programme names shown on EACEA's catalogue, not a claim that any one of them has an open scholarship on 16 November 2026.

The applicant should open each consortium website, compare degree prerequisites, teaching language, residence and mobility requirements, and look for 2027 entry dates. An applicant with strong software-engineering coursework might have a different shortlist from an applicant specialising in public policy or health data. Write down why each course is a good fit; do not submit to a programme solely because the catalogue contains your keyword.

When should you apply for the September 2027 intake?

The European Commission says most Erasmus Mundus master's applications fall between October and January for courses beginning the following academic year. For September 2027, many relevant course windows will therefore fall in late 2026 or early 2027, but there is no single Erasmus Mundus deadline. Dates for a course, a scholarship application and later self-funded admissions can differ.

Save the direct programme link and its current admissions page beside the deadline. If a site still shows 2026 entry, do not silently turn its date into a 2027 deadline. Check the course's latest notice or contact the consortium. Our deadline tracker helps separate university admissions, funding, references and nomination steps.

Before you apply, answer these six questions

  1. Does the official EACEA catalogue actually include the named Erasmus Mundus Joint Master?
  2. Can I find the current consortium website and the entry year I intend to join?
  3. Does my previous degree satisfy its subject, grade and language requirements?
  4. Does this specific intake offer an Erasmus Mundus scholarship, and what does it cover?
  5. Where will I study, and are there extra travel, visa or residency conditions?
  6. What is the official application deadline, time zone and portal, and has the consortium confirmed receipt?

Use the Erasmus Mundus application guide for document preparation and scholarship eligibility guide for your wider checklist. All application availability and country rules must still be verified at the consortium.

How to use the Erasmus Mundus catalogue for a 2027 application

Start from the European Education and Culture Executive Agency's Erasmus Mundus catalogue. Search by subject and compare the actual joint master's courses listed there. A catalogue entry describes a programme: it is not proof that scholarships are funded or that applications for your preferred intake are already open.

  1. Open the specific consortium website from the catalogue. Record the lead institution, partner universities and study locations.
  2. Read the current admissions criteria: degree subjects, prerequisites, accepted transcripts, references and language evidence. These can differ substantially between courses.
  3. Find the programme's current intake, admissions deadline, and whether the scholarship selection has a separate or earlier deadline. The European Commission says applications are commonly submitted between October and January, but the consortium sets the actual dates.
  4. Check whether this intake offers Erasmus Mundus scholarships, how many are available if stated, what expenses are supported and how any self-funded place differs.
  5. Shortlist only programmes you are academically eligible to join. Build a comparison of course fit, countries of study, application route, verified funding and deadlines.

Erasmus Mundus catalogue questions

Do I apply directly to the Erasmus Mundus catalogue?

No. You normally use the catalogue to discover a joint master's programme and apply to the institution or consortium that runs it. The consortium publishes the course-specific requirements and application steps.

Does every Erasmus Mundus Joint Master offer a fully funded scholarship every year?

No blanket guarantee can be made for every catalogue listing and intake. The programme's own current-year application page must confirm scholarship availability and financial support. A programme may have admissions options without offering a funded place to every student.

What subjects can I study?

The catalogue covers different disciplines. Search for programmes matching your completed degree, then read each course's specific prerequisite subjects. A relevant keyword match alone is not enough to establish academic eligibility.

Compare the separate Erasmus Mundus scholarship application guide and the master's scholarships hub when preparing a shortlist.

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters catalogue: how to choose courses

The Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters catalogue helps you discover joint master's programmes. It is not one central scholarship application, and a course's presence in the catalogue does not prove a funded scholarship is open for the current intake. Open each consortium's official site to check eligibility, language requirements, partner universities, mobility structure, tuition, scholarships and application deadlines.

Shortlist courses by academic fit first, then verify the current admission and funding round. Use the European Commission's programme guidance and the consortium's own admissions page.
