Erasmus Mundus Course Catalogue: How to Shortlist Joint Master's Degrees

Select actual Erasmus Mundus courses before assessing scholarships, mobility and deadlines.

OppMatcher editorial · Evidence-first applications23 October 2026
Quick answerThe European Commission's Erasmus Mundus course catalogue helps you discover joint master's degrees, but you apply directly to the consortium offering your chosen degree. Check the course website for its current admissions and scholarship rules. There is no single application form for every programme.

Select actual Erasmus Mundus courses before assessing scholarships, mobility and deadlines. This is an evergreen decision guide. Always confirm the latest application status, scheme conditions and closing time on the official page.

Start from the programme catalogue

Erasmus Mundus is a family of joint master's degrees run by university consortia, not a single subject or scholarship form. Identify courses matching your previous study and future goals. Open the consortium page for each one and record participating institutions and the degree award.

Check admissions before the scholarship

A programme may be academically open to candidates who are not selected for funding. Confirm academic prerequisites, language requirements, required references and the separate scholarship rules. Avoid assuming any consortium in the catalogue offers a scholarship for the intake you want.

Consider mobility and city costs

Some courses require study in two or more countries. Check the intended sequence of campuses, academic start dates and immigration steps. Housing costs and travel between universities can affect affordability even with funding, and the consortium should explain how the mobility arrangement works.

Create a useful shortlist

Compare three real course options in a table covering fit, host locations, scholarship selection and submission route. Eliminate a course if a mandatory entry condition is not met. Then request transcripts and references based on the most demanding application checklist rather than relying on one generic form.

Practical example

Example: a computer science graduate considering public policy and data programmes should compare the entry modules and expected quantitative background before choosing which Erasmus Mundus consortia are realistic.

How to use the Erasmus Mundus catalogue for a 2027 application

Start from the European Education and Culture Executive Agency's Erasmus Mundus catalogue. Search by subject and compare the actual joint master's courses listed there. A catalogue entry describes a programme: it is not proof that scholarships are funded or that applications for your preferred intake are already open.

  1. Open the specific consortium website from the catalogue. Record the lead institution, partner universities and study locations.
  2. Read the current admissions criteria: degree subjects, prerequisites, accepted transcripts, references and language evidence. These can differ substantially between courses.
  3. Find the programme's current intake, admissions deadline, and whether the scholarship selection has a separate or earlier deadline. The European Commission says applications are commonly submitted between October and January, but the consortium sets the actual dates.
  4. Check whether this intake offers Erasmus Mundus scholarships, how many are available if stated, what expenses are supported and how any self-funded place differs.
  5. Shortlist only programmes you are academically eligible to join. Build a comparison of course fit, countries of study, application route, verified funding and deadlines.

Erasmus Mundus catalogue questions

Do I apply directly to the Erasmus Mundus catalogue?

No. You normally use the catalogue to discover a joint master's programme and apply to the institution or consortium that runs it. The consortium publishes the course-specific requirements and application steps.

Does every Erasmus Mundus Joint Master offer a fully funded scholarship every year?

No blanket guarantee can be made for every catalogue listing and intake. The programme's own current-year application page must confirm scholarship availability and financial support. A programme may have admissions options without offering a funded place to every student.

What subjects can I study?

The catalogue covers different disciplines. Search for programmes matching your completed degree, then read each course's specific prerequisite subjects. A relevant keyword match alone is not enough to establish academic eligibility.
