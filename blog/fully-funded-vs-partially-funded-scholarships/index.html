Fully Funded vs Partially Funded Scholarships: Compare the Real Cost

Learn to compare tuition waivers, stipends and uncovered expenses without relying on marketing labels.

OppMatcher editorial · Research and application guide23 October 2026

Learn to compare tuition waivers, stipends and uncovered expenses without relying on marketing labels. The goal is to check the exact rules of the programme you intend to use, not to treat any general guide as a confirmed open application.

Make a cost-of-study worksheet

List tuition, housing, food, health cover, books, course fees, travel and residence-document costs. Estimate expenses using the intended university and city rather than a generic national average. Then record what the scholarship covers under each category, using the provider's exact wording.

Separate a waiver from cash support

A fee waiver can be valuable but may provide nothing for rent or arrival costs. A monthly maintenance award may support living costs without paying tuition. Check whether a scholarship pays the institution directly, reimburses expenses, or transfers money to the student after enrolment.

Read conditions that affect the total

Some awards have renewal requirements, performance conditions, part-time restrictions or limits on the number of funded terms. A master's award for one academic year may not cover a longer placement or resit. Read the funding conditions rather than estimating from the largest advertised annual amount.

Compare net cost, not the badge

Calculate the difference between expected eligible expenses and confirmed support. Include the first month's costs and any required deposit before payments begin. A smaller award in an affordable city may be more useful than a prestigious tuition discount combined with expensive housing.

Worked example

Example: Scholarship A covers 100% of tuition but no living costs. Scholarship B covers 60% of tuition and offers monthly housing support. A fair comparison requires the local tuition bill, course length, housing cost and payment schedule; neither label alone identifies the better option.

Action checklist

  1. Write the net cost in the host currency
  2. Mark each benefit as confirmed or unknown
  3. Check renewal and payment conditions

Frequently asked question

Does 'fully funded' always cover flights?

No. The specific award terms control travel, insurance and other costs.
