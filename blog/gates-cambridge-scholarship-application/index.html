Gates Cambridge: Postgraduate Scholarship Application

Understand the relationship between Cambridge admission and Gates Cambridge funding.

OppMatcher editorialPublished 23 October 2026

Understand the relationship between Cambridge admission and Gates Cambridge funding. This guide explains a programme route and does not claim that applications are currently open.

Choose an eligible Cambridge degree

Not every Cambridge graduate course is eligible for Gates Cambridge. Read the scheme's exclusion list and check the academic requirements of your proposed course. A suitable funding route does not guarantee that the department will offer admission.

Track the connected applications

Gates Cambridge is connected with the University's postgraduate application process. Review which references, essays and funding questions are required for the relevant applicant route. Give each form and recommendation deadline its own place in your checklist.

Explain your intellectual direction

Describe what you have worked on, which questions still matter and why a particular Cambridge programme is suited to them. Evidence from research, work or community initiatives should support your claims. Avoid spending half the statement praising the university without discussing your own contribution.

Check the correct applicant timetable

Funding timings can differ by applicant location and course. Use the official guide to confirm the relevant cycle and your submission time zone. Do not assume a general Cambridge course deadline is identical to the Gates Cambridge funding deadline.

Before submitting your application

Make a copy of the current official call, record the programme name and route, and confirm the time zone of any stated deadline. Check nationality, degree level, language requirements and the documents needed for your particular programme. If the relevant details are missing, contact the provider rather than treating the programme as open or as a guaranteed personal match.
