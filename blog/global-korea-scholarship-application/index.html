Global Korea Scholarship (GKS): Graduate and Embassy Routes

Use the official GKS notice to compare degree levels, routes and document rules.

OppMatcher editorialPublished 23 October 2026

Use the official GKS notice to compare degree levels, routes and document rules. This guide explains a programme route and does not claim that applications are currently open.

Find the right year's announcement

Study in Korea provides government scholarship information, but the rules can differ between undergraduate and graduate intakes. Open the current notice and its application guidelines. Do not use a prior year's document checklist as if it were valid now.

Check the application route

Embassy and university pathways have different submission procedures and restrictions. Determine which is permitted for your nationality and intended programme. Be especially careful about rules that limit the number of institutions or departments you may choose.

Plan language and degree evidence

A university may teach in Korean or English and may impose additional language conditions. Read the current GKS guidance for academic records, graduation proof, citizenship documents and certifications. Allow time for official translations or verification.

Make the programme choice practical

Compare degree curriculum, research fit, city, living conditions and any language training. A scholarship name does not tell you whether the actual department is suitable. Use the university's own course website as a second primary source.

Before submitting your application

Make a copy of the current official call, record the programme name and route, and confirm the time zone of any stated deadline. Check nationality, degree level, language requirements and the documents needed for your particular programme. If the relevant details are missing, contact the provider rather than treating the programme as open or as a guaranteed personal match.
