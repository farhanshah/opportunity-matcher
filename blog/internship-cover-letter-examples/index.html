Internship cover letter examples for students

Connect experience to the exact vacancy

How to approach it

Name the employer, team and vacancy. Refer to the posted duties rather than writing a letter intended for every internship.

If you have little formal experience, explain authentic coursework, student projects, volunteering or part-time work. Highlight a specific task, method and outcome without claiming professional responsibility you never held.

Read the provider's actual application instructions. Some portals use screening fields and do not require a letter. Check nationality, work permissions and duty-station restrictions separately.

Practical checklist

  1. Open the named official provider's current application instructions.
  2. Confirm nationality, residence, degree and other hard rules.
  3. Record supporting documents, time zone and submission process.
