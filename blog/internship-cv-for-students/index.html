Internship CV for Students 2027: Example Layout

Show relevant projects without inventing experience

What the application asks you to show

A student internship CV should make it easy to check whether you meet the actual vacancy's requirements. Many internships welcome candidates without previous employment, but their coursework, enrolment date, language skills or work permission may still be mandatory.

How to structure the evidence

Start with contact information, the relevant degree and expected completion date. Put a concise project section next, with the tools used, what you contributed and a verifiable deliverable. In a software role, a small documented code project can be more useful than unrelated part-time tasks. For a policy role, a research brief and evidence of writing may be stronger.

An example to adapt truthfully

Example project bullet, for illustration only: Cleaned a sample dataset in Python, documented missing values and summarised results in a three-page report. Replace it with work you actually did, and never copy an employer's confidential material into your public portfolio.

Before you submit

Check the employer's format, location and work-authorisation requirements. Do not attach a photograph or personal information unless appropriate in the recruitment market and requested. Use headings that applicant-tracking systems can parse, and keep the role title and organisation accurate.

  1. Reopen the actual provider's current application requirements.
  2. Check nationality, academic stage and work permission where applicable.
  3. Use only verifiable examples and follow the document format.
