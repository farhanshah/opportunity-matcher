Internships After Graduation: How to Check Recent-Graduate Eligibility

The word graduate can refer to a qualification, a time window or an employer's own candidate category.

OppMatcher editorial · Application guidance23 October 2026

The word graduate can refer to a qualification, a time window or an employer's own candidate category. Use this guide to evaluate a specific posting against its official evidence; it is not a guarantee that any particular internship or selection process is currently open.

Read the graduation clock precisely

Some programmes admit candidates only within a specified period after their most recent degree. Others require active enrolment throughout the internship. The definition is set by the provider, not by how you describe yourself. Calculate the relevant date against the stated application or placement-start date.

Check degree level and field

A recent bachelor's graduate may qualify for one internship but not a programme that requires ongoing master's study. Read subject restrictions and minimum years of university education as separate conditions. Do not infer acceptance from a generic 'graduates welcome' headline.

Confirm work authorisation and contract type

Graduate status does not automatically confer the right to work in the duty-station country. Remote and freelance labels can have their own conditions. If you are in the UK on a time-limited immigration permission, check the authorised work activities and employer's conditions for the relevant start date.

Avoid expired application pages

An old university career page may remain indexed after an internship cycle closes. Save an exact requisition link and check the date last updated. An evergreen talent pool does not guarantee an active placement or a start date.

Worked example

Example: someone graduates in January and finds a December internship. They should compare the programme's recent-graduate window with the actual start date and confirm whether the school must still certify student status.

Before applying

  1. Calculate graduate eligibility on the specified reference date
  2. Read degree and subject requirements
  3. Verify visa and work conditions

Frequently asked question

Can I apply for an internship after graduating?

Sometimes. Some employers accept recent graduates, while other internships require active university enrolment throughout the placement. Check the exact vacancy's graduation window, country eligibility, work permission and contract terms before applying.

How long after graduation can you still apply?

There is no universal “recent graduate” definition. It can be a year, two years or no graduate route at all. Compare the UNICEF internship requirements, UNDP FAQ and OECD internship programme.

Example: the application date is not the eligibility date

Imagine a student finished their degree on 30 June 2026. A UNDP internship beginning on 1 May 2027 might fall within the published one-year recent-graduate window, assuming other conditions are met. A start on 1 August 2027 would generally fall outside that rule. Merely submitting an application in March 2027 would not make an August start eligible.

For a UNICEF internship, the general two-year graduation window can allow a later start, but the provider's actual notice takes priority. In contrast, the OECD programme's continuing full-time student requirement means a fully graduated applicant may need an alternative route.

What if you're awaiting formal certificates?

Degree completion, degree conferral and certificate issue dates are sometimes different. Ask the employer what date it considers your completion date and what proof it accepts. A university confirmation of successful completion may be useful where permitted, but a provisional letter is not automatically equivalent to the certificate demanded by every programme.

Likewise, moving immediately into a master's programme can change your student status. Record the actual programme enrolment and term dates before claiming eligibility as a master's student.

A graduate window does not establish work permission

Applicants still need any applicable right to work or take part in an internship at the destination. Even a remote role may specify legal residence or local payroll restrictions. Check the immigration authority and employer, particularly if the placement crosses national borders.
