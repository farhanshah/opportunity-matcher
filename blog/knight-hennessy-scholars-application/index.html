Knight-Hennessy Scholars: Stanford Application Checklist

Build a separate plan for graduate admission and Knight-Hennessy funding.

OppMatcher editorialPublished 23 October 2026

Build a separate plan for graduate admission and Knight-Hennessy funding. This guide explains a programme route and does not claim that applications are currently open.

Plan two independent submissions

Stanford graduate programmes and Knight-Hennessy Scholars assess different things. Confirm the eligible degree first, then create separate submission checklists for the department and scholarship. An excellent funding application cannot replace the university's academic review.

Check the first-degree timing rules

The programme's graduation window matters. Compare the date of your first bachelor's degree with the official eligibility period for the actual cohort. Also review any eligible-degree restrictions and departmental test or portfolio requirements.

Use evidence of real initiative

Choose a project where you solved a problem, brought people together or changed a process. Describe your actions and what you learned, rather than listing every position on your CV. Be accurate about what your team accomplished and what you personally did.

Prepare for differing review stages

A scholarship process may include supplementary evaluation or conversations, while a graduate programme may ask for references and academic evidence. Keep decisions, acceptances and funding steps separate so an outstanding task is not missed.

Before submitting your application

Make a copy of the current official call, record the programme name and route, and confirm the time zone of any stated deadline. Check nationality, degree level, language requirements and the documents needed for your particular programme. If the relevant details are missing, contact the provider rather than treating the programme as open or as a guaranteed personal match.
