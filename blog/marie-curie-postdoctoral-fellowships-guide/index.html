MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships: Host and Mobility Checks

A postdoctoral MSCA proposal involves a researcher and an eligible hosting institution.

OppMatcher editorialPublished 23 October 2026Updated 3 November 2026 with official programme evidence

A postdoctoral MSCA proposal involves a researcher and an eligible hosting institution. This guide explains a programme route and does not claim that applications are currently open.

Check the right EU call

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions supports several types of research opportunities. Postdoctoral fellowships have their own mobility, doctoral and experience requirements. Read the current official call and guide to applicants rather than using a general European funding page.

Find a genuinely suitable host

Start with researchers whose publications or facilities fit your proposed question. Discuss supervision, infrastructure and institutional support before investing in the proposal. A host should understand the research plan and their responsibilities, not just agree to appear on a form.

Describe the training benefit

The application is about more than completing a research project. Explain the techniques, supervision, knowledge exchange and professional development that make the placement useful. Be precise about the outputs that could be achieved in the available period.

Understand the application pathway

The EU guidance describes submission through the host organisation in the funding portal. A vacancy advertised by a university can be different from a new fellowship grant proposal. Confirm which one you are pursuing and follow its exact documents and deadline.

Before submitting your application

Save the official vacancy or programme link, record the current application status and verify the deadline and time zone. Check nationality, academic level, language conditions and any employment or residence limits. If the information is missing, do not assume you qualify. Contact the provider rather than acting on an outdated copy of the call.

The 2026 call closed; the next scheduled opening is April 2027

According to the European Commission's MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2026 call, applications opened on 9 April 2026 and closed on 9 September 2026 at 17:00 CEST. The main Postdoctoral Fellowships programme page lists the next call as scheduled to open on 7 April 2027. As of 3 November 2026, do not describe the 2026 call as open.

European and Global Postdoctoral Fellowships are not interchangeable

NationalityResearchers of any nationality, subject to other eligibility rules.Nationals or long-term residents of EU Member States or Horizon Europe Associated Countries.

Example: a researcher of a non-EU nationality can potentially apply for a European Fellowship with an eligible European host. The same nationality alone does not qualify the researcher for a Global Fellowship unless the applicable long-term residence rule is met.

PhD experience and mobility checks

The programme generally requires a PhD at the call deadline, including researchers who have successfully defended their thesis but have not yet formally received the award. The standard experience limit is eight years of research experience after the PhD, with specific exclusions for qualifying career breaks and non-research periods.

The mobility rule is a separate requirement. Applicants must not have lived or worked or studied in the relevant host country for more than 12 months during the 36 months before the call deadline, using the appropriate host for the European or Global route. See the Commission's six-step preparation guidance before calculating dates. An approximate memory of when someone moved countries is not reliable enough for this rule.

Worked example: counting the mobility period

Suppose a researcher's intended host is in Spain, and they spent 10 months there during the three years immediately before the deadline. They may be within the basic 12/36-month limit, but need to calculate all qualifying residence and activity periods. If the same person spent 14 months there during that window, they would generally exceed the threshold for the relevant European host-country check unless an exception applied. The official call and mobility guidance determine which activities count.

A real host partnership and an accurate funding budget

MSCA applications are prepared with a host organisation, not submitted as a personal grant without an eligible beneficiary. The organisation must be able to recruit the fellow for the funded period. The funding supports living and mobility allowances, with possible family or special-needs support, plus research, training, networking and management costs. Funding is paid through the beneficiary organisation; a headline grant amount is not automatically the employee's take-home pay.
