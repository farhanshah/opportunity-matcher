Master's Scholarships for International Students: How to Compare Offers

Build a shortlist based on citizenship, admission requirements and the actual funding package.

OppMatcher editorial · Research and application guide23 October 2026
Editorial reviewThis shorter guide is being reviewed for more detailed evidence. For updated, source-backed information, read our related programme guide and confirm any application decision at the official provider. Do not treat an older deadline or eligibility summary here as a current open call.

Build a shortlist based on citizenship, admission requirements and the actual funding package. The goal is to check the exact rules of the programme you intend to use, not to treat any general guide as a confirmed open application.

Begin with the programme, not the headline award

An international master's scholarship can fund a specific course, institution or set of subjects. Choose the degree you would genuinely study, then look for awards attached to it. A large scholarship list is not useful if the university will not accept your current qualification or the subject does not fit your plans.

Check three eligibility decisions

Ask whether your citizenship qualifies, whether your academic record meets the degree-entry requirements, and whether the scholarship is open to the type of master's programme you want. Some awards need previous professional experience or nomination from a country organisation. Record an official citation for each yes; an unanswered question remains uncertain.

Compare money by expense and time

Break the award into tuition assistance, monthly living payments, travel, insurance and other items. Check whether benefits last for the entire course, whether funds are paid after arrival, and whether dependent costs are excluded. If an award includes only a tuition discount, do not describe it as fully funded.

Track two or more application systems

University admission and scholarship selection may be separate, even when they share one institution. Write down the application account, referee deadline and supporting documents for each. If you cannot get a referee or transcript on time, an otherwise attractive scholarship may not be realistic for that intake.

Worked example

Example: compare a joint European master's programme with a national government-funded master's. The first might involve multiple host universities and mobility; the second might involve a separate nomination agency. Their country eligibility and submission routes must be verified individually.

Action checklist

  1. Make a shortlist of three degree programmes
  2. Calculate uncovered expenses for each award
  3. Identify who receives the scholarship application

Frequently asked question

Are master's scholarships open to every nationality?

No. Each provider sets its own eligibility and application route.
