PhD Fellowship vs Research Grant: Funding Differences Explained

Understand whether an award funds your own doctoral study, a supervisor's project or a research institution.

OppMatcher editorial · Research and application guide23 October 2026

Understand whether an award funds your own doctoral study, a supervisor's project or a research institution. The goal is to check the exact rules of the programme you intend to use, not to treat any general guide as a confirmed open application.

Find the applicant named in the scheme

A doctoral fellowship may assess you as an individual student, while a research grant can be submitted by a principal investigator or university. Read the eligibility section and identify who signs the contract. A scheme for postdoctoral researchers does not automatically fund someone who has not yet earned a doctorate.

Understand what the money actually pays

Look at tuition, salary or stipend, employer costs, equipment, research travel and overheads as separate categories. A laboratory grant worth a large amount is not necessarily personal funding. Confirm whether a stipend is tax-free or a salary subject to local rules, based on the actual agreement.

Check the host and supervision requirements

Funding may depend on an approved supervisor, named project, host institution or particular type of research environment. Contact the potential supervisor with a concise research question, preparation and funding route. Do not promise an institutional commitment the department has not offered.

Work backwards from research milestones

Research proposals, ethics approvals, institutional sign-off and recommendation letters can all delay applications. A doctoral applicant should also know whether admission must come first. Prepare an evidence trail for each requirement and allow a realistic period for the university to approve the application.

Worked example

Example: a candidate interested in climate adaptation may find a PhD studentship advertised by a department and an open postdoctoral grant from the same funding body. Only the first is likely suitable before finishing doctoral training.

Action checklist

  1. Confirm the degree stage required
  2. Record who receives and spends the funding
  3. Check required host and supervisor endorsement

Frequently asked question

Can I use a postdoctoral fellowship to start a PhD?

Normally these are different career stages. Check the specific programme.
