Scholarship CV Template for International Students 2027

A practical academic CV layout

What the application asks you to show

A scholarship CV is an evidence document. It is designed for an admissions or award committee deciding whether you meet stated criteria, not a generic online résumé. The sections that matter depend on whether the award values academic performance, research, leadership or professional contribution.

How to structure the evidence

A straightforward two-page structure begins with your name and reliable contact details, followed by education, relevant research or academic projects, work or volunteering, publications or awards where genuine, and technical or language evidence. Put the strongest qualifying experience nearest the top. If the provider requests a one-page CV, reduce weaker material instead of shrinking the font.

An example to adapt truthfully

Example entry: University research project, September–December 2025. Analysed publicly available survey data using spreadsheet models; wrote a methods summary and presented two limitations to a class audience. This is an illustrative sentence, not an achievement you should copy if it is untrue.

Before you submit

Check the scholarship's official format and number-of-pages rules. A standard academic CV differs from a Europass form or a university portal's fixed résumé field. Do not invent publications, inflate involvement, or list training that cannot be verified.

  1. Reopen the actual provider's current application requirements.
  2. Check nationality, academic stage and work permission where applicable.
  3. Use only verifiable examples and follow the document format.
