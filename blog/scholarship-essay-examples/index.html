Scholarship Essay Examples: Evidence-Based Structures

Examples for leadership, motivation and academic goals

What the application asks you to show

A scholarship essay should answer the particular question that the organiser asks. The following outlines are hypothetical teaching examples, not submissions from successful applicants and not text to paste into your own application. Every fact used in the final essay must be yours.

How to structure the evidence

Motivation example structure: I developed an interest in water quality while measuring samples in a university lab. My final-year project showed me the limits of the methods I used. The named course offers a module and research group that could help me learn the appropriate techniques. Each sentence should be replaced with a truthful, course-specific observation.

An example to adapt truthfully

Leadership example structure: A student group had inconsistent task deadlines. I proposed a shared timetable, assigned responsibility with agreement from the group and reviewed the results after the event. The reflection should explain what was difficult and how you would act differently, not simply claim that you are a natural leader.

Before you submit

Research-potential example structure: identify a specific question, describe the evidence you already worked with and explain why the sponsor's facilities or supervisors could help answer the next question. Do not describe an unapproved project as funded or claim a faculty member has agreed to host you.

  1. Reopen the actual provider's current application requirements.
  2. Check nationality, academic stage and work permission where applicable.
  3. Use only verifiable examples and follow the document format.
