Scholarship motivation letter structure and example

Write a programme-specific explanation

How to approach it

A motivation letter works best when it names one academic or professional objective and connects it to the exact course. Avoid generic claims that would suit any scholarship.

Use authentic coursework, community work or projects to explain what you learned and what further training will allow you to do. The purpose is to show a plausible next step, not to promise impossible outcomes.

Read the current application prompts and word count before submitting. Some providers ask for a personal statement, leadership essay or research proposal instead of a standard motivation letter.

Practical checklist

  1. Open the named official provider's current application instructions.
  2. Confirm nationality, residence, degree and other hard rules.
  3. Record supporting documents, time zone and submission process.
