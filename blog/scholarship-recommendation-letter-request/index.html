How to Request a Scholarship Recommendation Letter

Give a referee the context, deadlines and supporting facts they need to write an accurate letter.

OppMatcher editorial · Research and application guide23 October 2026
Editorial reviewThis shorter guide is being reviewed for more detailed evidence. For updated, source-backed information, read our related guide and check the actual provider before applying. Earlier dates and general conditions here must not be assumed current.

Give a referee the context, deadlines and supporting facts they need to write an accurate letter. The goal is to check the exact rules of the programme you intend to use, not to treat any general guide as a confirmed open application.

Choose a referee who knows your work

A famous title is less useful than a person who can discuss your academic progress, project work or leadership with real examples. Check the scholarship's rule for academic versus professional referees before approaching someone.

Make the request specific

State the award, intended course, closing date, submission method and reasons you have chosen this referee. Attach a current CV and a short description of relevant work you completed with them. Avoid asking someone to sign a letter you wrote under their name.

Explain the submission mechanics

Some portals email a unique upload link directly to the referee. Other schemes ask for a letter on institutional stationery. Confirm which process applies and whether the reference is confidential. Send your referee clear instructions, including whether the deadline is in a foreign time zone.

Follow up with reasonable notice

Send a reminder before the internal deadline, not ten messages on the closing day. Thank the referee after submission and confirm the portal received the letter. If a reference becomes impossible, contact the programme about the approved alternative rather than uploading a fabricated one.

Worked example

Example request: 'I'm applying for the Master's funding programme at [university]. Could you describe my research methods and contribution to our final-year project? The referee portal closes on [date/time], and I can provide the project report and CV.'

Action checklist

  1. Confirm referee category
  2. Share the exact prompt and secure submission instructions
  3. Track upload confirmation separately from your application

Frequently asked question

Can my relative write the reference?

Most academic programmes expect independent referees. Check the scheme's conflict-of-interest rule.
