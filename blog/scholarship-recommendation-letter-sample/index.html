Scholarship recommendation letter and referee checklist

Help referees provide reliable evidence

How to approach it

A referee should describe direct observations of the candidate's academic, professional or research work. Specific methods, responsibilities and examples are more useful than broad praise.

Share the award criteria, actual deadline and a short list of projects the referee can verify. Do not write an invented letter for someone to sign as their own assessment.

Check whether the referee must submit through a provider portal, use an institutional email, or upload an explicitly signed document. Submission requirements vary by programme.

Practical checklist

  1. Open the named official provider's current application instructions.
  2. Confirm nationality, residence, degree and other hard rules.
  3. Record supporting documents, time zone and submission process.

A guide is a research aid, not evidence of an open application or your eligibility. The provider's current documents control the final decision.
