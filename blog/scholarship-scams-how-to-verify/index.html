How to verify a scholarship and avoid scams

Verify sponsors and application routes

How to approach it

A scholarship logo or message cannot establish that a sender represents the sponsoring organisation. Look up the programme on the organiser's own website and compare the award title, eligible countries and current submission route.

Treat requests for payments to release an award, account passwords or identity documents sent to personal email addresses as warning signs. An offer that appears only on an aggregator is not proof of an active official scholarship.

If the provider has not published the call, record it as unverified and contact the organiser using contact information from its own domain. Do not accept an urgency claim as proof of authenticity.

Practical checklist

  1. Open the named official provider's current application instructions.
  2. Confirm nationality, residence, degree and other hard rules.
  3. Record supporting documents, time zone and submission process.
