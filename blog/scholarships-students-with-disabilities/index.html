Scholarships for Students With Disabilities: Accessibility and Eligibility Questions

Check the award's eligibility wording, accessibility procedures and reasonable adjustment process.

OppMatcher editorial · Evidence-first applications23 October 2026
Editorial reviewThis shorter guide is being reviewed for more detailed evidence. For updated, source-backed information, read our related guide and check the actual provider before applying. Earlier dates and general conditions here must not be assumed current.

Check the award's eligibility wording, accessibility procedures and reasonable adjustment process. This is an evergreen decision guide. Always confirm the latest application status, scheme conditions and closing time on the official page.

Check the scholarship's actual purpose

Some awards explicitly support applicants with disabilities; others are open to broader groups but have accessibility procedures. An inclusion statement does not itself create a disability-specific scholarship. Read the named programme and its current conditions.

Separate eligibility from sensitive disclosure

You should not send medical records or disability information to an unofficial aggregator. Providers may explain what evidence, if any, is required and how they handle it. Ask the scholarship office through a verified channel about accessible forms and reasonable adjustments.

Plan academic and living support

Consider accessible accommodation, assistive technology, support services and additional transport costs. Check which expenses an award covers and what help the university can provide separately. A stipend may not automatically cover specialist equipment.

Request adjustments in time

If interviews, tests or application portals create access barriers, contact the authorised programme team before the deadline. Describe the adjustment needed without providing unnecessary sensitive detail. Save the response and any updated procedure in your application tracker.

Practical example

Example: an applicant needs an accessible remote interview rather than a particular travel arrangement. They should contact the scholarship office with the specific access request and confirm how the provider handles the process.

What to check

  1. Find the specific award eligibility text
  2. Use official channels for adjustments
  3. Compare funding with accessibility expenses
