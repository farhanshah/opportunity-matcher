UK Internships for International Graduates: How to Assess Eligibility

Check contract restrictions, work permission and graduate recruitment deadlines for UK-based roles.

OppMatcher editorial · Application guidance23 October 2026

Check contract restrictions, work permission and graduate recruitment deadlines for UK-based roles. Use this guide to evaluate a specific posting against its official evidence; it is not a guarantee that any particular internship or selection process is currently open.

Check the employer's candidate requirements

Some UK programmes are designed for students undertaking a sandwich placement, some for new graduates and others for any applicant with the required skills. Read the qualification stage and course-enrolment rules instead of assuming all internships accept graduates.

Interpret sponsorship questions carefully

Employers may ask whether you currently have work authorisation and whether you might require sponsorship later. Those are separate facts. Answer according to your immigration position, role duration and the wording of the question; do not infer eligibility from a generic university careers article.

Compare working terms and costs

Ask about hourly pay, location, expected days on site, probation and whether the position is genuinely a fixed-term development role. If you would relocate within the UK, include rent and commuting costs in the decision, particularly where a role is short.

Build applications around local evidence

Describe teamwork, academic projects, customer service or technical outputs that match the vacancy. Check whether the company accepts overseas qualifications and whether it has a formal deadline or rolling recruitment. Keep applications truthful and save confirmations.

Worked example

Example: a graduate in Middlesbrough considers a six-month placement in London. They should compare net pay after housing and travel with the role's learning value and verify that their own work permission covers the proposed contract.

Before applying

  1. Confirm student versus graduate eligibility
  2. Answer work permission questions truthfully
  3. Compare location costs and genuine learning
