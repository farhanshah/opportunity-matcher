UN Internship Application Checklist: From Vacancy to Submission

Identify the exact UN vacancy, confirm student eligibility and prepare evidence before its closing date.

OppMatcher editorial · Application guidance23 October 2026

Identify the exact UN vacancy, confirm student eligibility and prepare evidence before its closing date. Use this guide to evaluate a specific posting against its official evidence; it is not a guarantee that any particular internship or selection process is currently open.

Find the employing organisation

United Nations vacancies appear through different agencies and recruitment platforms. A UN internship at UNICEF is not applied for through the same system as every UNDP or WHO role. Save the organisation, requisition code, office and closing date from the official notice. Programme overviews explain general eligibility but are not current job advertisements.

Check academic and location restrictions

Inspect whether the posting requires current enrolment, a particular graduation period, a nationality, or residency at the duty station. Some roles are locally recruited and some allow remote work only within a specified country. Do not submit simply because the title includes 'international'.

Match qualifications to the posted duties

Create a two-column list with each required skill and a genuine example from coursework, volunteering or work. For research, include a method and result; for programme support, describe coordination or documentation. If you do not meet a mandatory requirement, do not conceal it with vague claims.

Complete the provider's own recruitment workflow

Check supporting-document formats, language requirements and referee fields. Save the application reference after submission. Never pay a recruiter for a United Nations role or send passport documents to an unverified personal email address.

Worked example

Example: a student sees 'Communication Intern' in a third-party search result. Before tailoring a résumé, they open the official agency page, confirm the job ID and country-specific eligibility, and record the exact closing time.

Before applying

  1. Check vacancy ID and official host
  2. Compare every mandatory eligibility condition
  3. Save application confirmation
