Placement Year vs Summer Internship: University Credit and Contract Checks

Understand what your degree institution needs before accepting a work placement.

OppMatcher editorial · Application guidance23 October 2026

Understand what your degree institution needs before accepting a work placement. Use this guide to evaluate a specific posting against its official evidence; it is not a guarantee that any particular internship or selection process is currently open.

Ask your programme about academic approval

A placement year may count towards a degree only if the university approves the employer, duration and type of work. A summer internship can be useful experience without automatically generating course credit. Request the institution's approved forms before accepting.

Distinguish academic and employment supervisors

A university may require an academic tutor, employer supervisor, reflective report or attendance assessment. Ask who is responsible for performance reviews and what happens if the placement ends early. Academic approval does not replace a valid employment contract.

Plan the practical details

Check insurance, pay, hours, travel and accommodation. International placements can require additional risk assessments and immigration permissions. The employer and university may use different calendars, so confirm when each commitment begins and ends.

Document outcomes ethically

Save permitted evidence of work completed for your portfolio or university report without disclosing confidential client or company information. If the employer imposes a confidentiality agreement, ask which projects you may describe publicly.

Worked example

Example: a student offered a marketing placement from June to August should check whether their university recognises that term as a formal placement year. A short summer placement may need a different assessment route.

Before applying

  1. Get written academic approval
  2. Confirm supervision and insurance
  3. Separate employment and course-assessment documents
