US Graduate Assistantship vs Fellowship: Tuition and Work Obligations

Compare assistantships that require work with fellowships and scholarships that have different terms.

Compare assistantships that require work with fellowships and scholarships that have different terms. This is an evergreen decision guide. Always confirm the latest application status, scheme conditions and closing time on the official page.

Understand assistantship duties

Teaching and research assistantships are often employment-linked awards involving work for a university department. Duties, hours and eligibility can vary. Read the graduate school's funding agreement and the department's appointment conditions before comparing it with an award that has no assigned work.

Check tuition and stipend separately

Some packages include tuition remission, a stipend and health benefits; others include only one component. Record whether university fees, summer months and insurance are included. Funding letters can be more precise than general statements on a university website.

Compare academic consequences

A teaching assistantship may develop classroom skills but consume time during coursework. A research assistantship can connect with a thesis project, but the assigned work may not be identical to your own research goals. Ask the department about workload and expectations.

Review international student restrictions

Employment and work-hour conditions can interact with immigration status. Check the university's international-student office and current US government guidance for your situation. Do not assume that funding or admission alone establishes permission for off-campus work.

Practical example

Example: two doctoral offers list similar annual support, but one pays 12 months with a research duty and the other pays only during the academic year. Compare net tuition, summer support and expected work before choosing.

What to check

  1. Record tuition remission and fees
  2. Check required work hours
  3. Ask about funding over summer

Common question

Is a US graduate assistantship the same as a fellowship?

Not necessarily. Assistantships often involve teaching or research responsibilities, while fellowships can have different award conditions. Compare work duties, tuition remission, stipend, duration and renewal rules.
