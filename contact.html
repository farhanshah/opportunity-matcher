Contact and corrections

Found a problem with a listing?

Tell us which page needs attention and what the official provider says. Specific corrections help us avoid sending applicants toward a closed call or the wrong eligibility rules.

Published contact address

OppMatcher currently publishes privacy@oppmatcher.tech as its contact address. You can use it to raise privacy questions and flag website or listing issues.

For a useful correction report, include: the OppMatcher page URL, the official provider URL, the exact inaccurate statement and the corrected wording or evidence. Please do not send identification documents, bank details, passwords, full applications or other sensitive personal data in an initial message.

Prepare a correction email

Incorrect deadline or status

Include a dated official notice or current provider link. An expired date should not remain labelled open just because an older search result still displays it.

Nationality or course restrictions

Point to the official rule and identify the affected country, degree, course or intake. A general programme page may not include every restriction.

Broken page or account issue

Describe what you tried, your browser and which URL failed. Do not include passwords, access tokens or private profile details.

Applications and scholarship decisions: Contact the university, employer or scholarship provider through its official website for application outcomes, appeals and admissions decisions. OppMatcher cannot intervene in those decisions.
