Build a deadline record from official evidence

Scholarship and internship applications can have several dates. A university admission deadline, a scholarship funding deadline, a referee submission deadline and a final vacancy closing date may belong to different systems. Missing any one of them can affect an otherwise eligible application. Do not copy the closing date from another country's edition of the programme or from a reposted advertisement.

Example, not a real opportunity: A hypothetical funding form closes on 20 March at 17:00 in the sponsor's stated time zone, while university admission closes on 1 March. Record both separately. Applying for the funding by 20 March would not undo a missed admission deadline. Never convert the time without first confirming the official time zone and whether daylight saving applies.

Check a deadline before acting on it
    

  1. Find the exact vacancy or programme intake on the provider's own site. An article repeating last year's dates is not sufficient.
    2. 

  2. Separate the opening date from the closing date. If the opening date is unavailable, label the application status unconfirmed.
    3. 

  3. Check which clock and time zone the provider uses. Do not assume that a date-only deadline means midnight in your own location.
    4. 

  4. Identify required nomination, references, exam scores or university admission steps that have an earlier cut-off.
    5. 

  5. Return to the provider before submission. An old screenshot or database check does not override the latest official announcement.
