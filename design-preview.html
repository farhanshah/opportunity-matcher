Explore our guides ↗
What is OppMatcher?

OppMatcher is an opportunity discovery and application workspace for scholarships, internships, fellowships and global programmes. It helps organize opportunities and consider profile-fit signals.

Does a match guarantee that I'm eligible?

No. Profile fit is guidance, not a decision by the programme provider. Country-specific conditions, deadlines and missing evidence should always be checked on the official source.

Where do the opportunities come from?

OppMatcher monitors registered official sources and researches additional opportunities worldwide. Search results stay unverified until they pass the relevant evidence checks.

Can I track my applications?

Yes. Your OppMatcher workspace helps you save opportunities, prepare application materials and keep track of application stages.

Is this the new live website already?

Not yet. You're viewing a standalone visual concept. The current OppMatcher homepage and its working features are unchanged while this design is being reviewed.
