This phase uses **photographs, not AI-generated scenes**, as illustrative images for scholarships, internships, fellowships, exchange programmes and career preparation. The pictured people are not applicants, awardees, employees, reviewers or endorsers of OppMatcher. Photos never establish programme eligibility or current opening status.

All image URLs in this initial phase use regular, free-to-use **Unsplash** photography. Free commercial use is allowed under https://unsplash.com/license (no mandatory credit), subject to third-party privacy, trademark and model rights. Never use an Unsplash+ paywalled image without an appropriate licence. Avoid apparent partner endorsements. These images are separate from official provider logos and official listing evidence.
