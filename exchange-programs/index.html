Application guidance

Before applying

Short courses, youth programmes and exchanges vary in funding. Some cover fees but not flights or housing. Check institution or organiser credentials, course credit, age rules, visa requirements and total costs. Check the actual sponsor, degree stage, citizenship and residence restrictions, then record each application stage separately.

  1. Check the named provider and current programme rules.
  2. Record eligibility, admission and the deadline time zone.
  3. Compare actual costs and funding components.
