International Volunteering and UN Volunteers: Programme Guide

Volunteer placements and online volunteering

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

International volunteering includes short local projects, professional UN volunteer assignments and online task-based programmes. These are different from university exchange programmes and should not be evaluated using the same funding assumptions.

Check the legal organiser, duties, safeguarding practices and financial arrangements. Online volunteering does not necessarily provide remuneration, a visa or a pathway to international employment. Field assignments can require experience and specific duty-station conditions.

The official UN Volunteers portal distinguishes opportunities and conditions. Confirm the posted assignment, expected time commitment and candidate eligibility rather than relying on an intermediary promising guaranteed selection.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
