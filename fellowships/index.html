Application guidance

Before applying

Fellowship programmes differ from degree scholarships. Host sponsorship, postdoctoral stage, professional experience and mobility rules may control eligibility. Compare the organiser's current guidance before writing a proposal. Check the actual sponsor, degree stage, citizenship and residence restrictions, then record each application stage separately.

  1. Check the named provider and current programme rules.
  2. Record eligibility, admission and the deadline time zone.
  3. Compare actual costs and funding components.

Use the free application checklist and browse current opportunities.

What kind of fellowship matches your background?

Research fellowships can depend on a doctoral award date, a proposed project and a supervisor willing to host the work. Leadership fellowships often focus on your professional decisions and community work. Policy and technology fellowships may ask for writing samples or a clearly defined research question. Applying to these as though they are ordinary tuition scholarships can waste time.

RouteEvidence to check
Postdoctoral researchDegree timing, mobility conditions, host institution and project methods
Professional leadershipExperience thresholds, recommendations and real examples of responsibility
Policy researchWriting or technical samples, subject requirements and residence restrictions

Start with the MSCA guide or UK research guide for route-specific examples.

Make a proposal possible to evaluate

Instead of saying you want to “improve public services,” define an outcome, a population, the evidence you can collect and a reasonable time frame. Explain what you can already do, what the host must support and which limitations are uncertain. Confirm whether the award pays salary, research costs or travel before creating a budget.

  1. Check that a specific provider call is currently accepting applications.
  2. Verify academic stage, experience, mobility and residence rules.
  3. Ask about host approval only through the official process.
  4. Prepare the research or professional evidence requested by the programme.
  5. Save the deadline and application confirmation.
