Before applying

Fellowship programmes differ from degree scholarships. Host sponsorship, postdoctoral stage, professional experience and mobility rules may control eligibility. Compare the organiser's current guidance before writing a proposal. Check the actual sponsor, degree stage, citizenship and residence restrictions, then record each application stage separately.

  1. Check the named provider and current programme rules.
  2. Record eligibility, admission and the deadline time zone.
  3. Compare actual costs and funding components.
