Policy, Climate and AI Fellowships 2027

Public-policy research and emerging technology

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

Policy and AI fellowships can be organised as visiting research, training, governance research or professional placements. Some programmes recruit early-career candidates while others require advanced research or policy experience.

AI governance opportunities may focus on technical evaluations, regulation, public administration or social consequences of emerging technology. A generic interest in AI is usually less useful than a demonstrated writing, analysis or research project.

Climate, journalism and human-rights fellowships may have fieldwork, location and output requirements. Before applying, review the organiser's purpose, proposed work, travel policy and whether you must hold permission to work in the host country.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
