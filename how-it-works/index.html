How OppMatcher checks scholarships, internships and fellowships

Discover opportunities, understand what the official source actually confirms and prepare your application without mistaking a general programme page for an open vacancy.

Source caution: A programme or guide is not confirmation of a current application. Verify deadlines, funding, and personal eligibility using the provider.

What the verification labels mean

OppMatcher monitors official provider websites and searches for individual application notices. A link found by a search engine is only a lead. Before an opening can be called verified, the recorded evidence must come from the actual provider, identify a specific opportunity and show a usable current application deadline. General scholarship pages and old announcements are not enough.

Recently verified individual vacancy

A specific provider posting has an identifiable title and official link, a future deadline and a recent successful check. This confirms the posting evidence, not that every visitor meets its nationality, degree, work-authorisation or language requirements.

Officially advertised application call

A named provider call may state that applications are being accepted within a date range. Its own dates and conditions need to be checked again; one open call does not mean every programme from that organisation is open.

Source-confirmed programme

The programme has a real official page, but the specific intake, closing date, application opening date or eligibility may still be missing. It remains useful research, not an invitation to submit an application today.

Closed or needs recheck

Expired deadlines are labelled closed. If a provider blocks access or its evidence becomes stale, an opportunity cannot keep an open label based only on old information. Historical detail pages remain available for reference.

Find the right type of opportunity

Scholarships for international students

Compare degree-level funding, destination and nationality rules before checking the official application route.

International internships for students and graduates

Check whether the opportunity is paid, where the work happens and whether an individual placement or candidate pool is advertised.

International research and professional fellowships

Review host-institution, career-stage and proposal requirements that can differ from ordinary graduate scholarships.

International exchange programmes

Compare programme duration, tuition or living-cost coverage, eligibility and the organiser's official application instructions.

What to check before applying

  1. Open the provider's own notice. Look for a distinct role, award or intake page, not a search summary or copied scholarship listing.
  2. Confirm the dates. An application deadline is not by itself proof that an intake has opened. Check the opening date, closing date and the provider's time zone. Rolling or university-specific deadlines require a separate check.
  3. Read restrictions in full. A programme based in a country is not necessarily open to that country's citizens or every international applicant. Check nationality, residence, age, degree timing, work experience, field and language rules.
  4. Check the funding wording. Fully funded, stipend, tuition waiver and unpaid placement mean different things. Compare what is covered with travel, insurance and living costs.
  5. Prepare evidence before submitting. Keep a record of the official link, required documents, references and any university or nominating-agency step.

What happens when a source is incomplete?

If the provider does not publish a clear closing date, status or eligibility conditions, OppMatcher keeps the lead in a research tier instead of inventing missing details. Some official sites restrict automated access or publish several overlapping application routes. A successful website fetch does not establish applicant eligibility, and a broken fetch does not prove a vacancy closed.

Read the verification methodology for the source hierarchy and freshness rules, the About page for the service's scope, or contact us to report a misleading status, missing country restriction or broken provider link.
