Internships in Europe 2027 for International Applicants

European placements and work permissions

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

Europe contains several different labour and student mobility systems. A paid placement in Switzerland, an EU-funded traineeship and an internship at a UK employer may have completely different residence, payroll and visa conditions.

Begin with the official vacancy. Check whether the employer recruits applicants outside the host country and whether the role is open to current students or graduates. The word European does not itself establish eligibility for non-EU citizens.

For research placements, check academic year and institutional sponsorship. For corporate roles, match the CV to the named requisition and document any work permit that the employer requires.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
