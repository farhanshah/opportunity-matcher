Paid internship, expense allowance or reimbursement?

The word “paid” can describe an hourly wage, a monthly stipend, travel reimbursement or an allowance for meals and transport. Those arrangements are not financially equivalent. An internship advertised with a stipend may require relocation, a deposit or separate insurance, while a salaried job can have deductions and different eligibility rules.

ArrangementWhat to verifyBudget consideration
Wage or salaryCurrency, pay period, expected hours and tax treatmentWhat remains after local living costs
Fixed stipendPayment timing, duration and what the amount coversRent, travel and other uncovered expenses
Expense reimbursementWhich receipts qualify and when claims are paidCash needed upfront
Unpaid academic placementStudy requirements, lawful arrangement and insurance coverageFull self-funded cost and course credit

Illustrative budget: If an internship allowance is 800 units per month and your documented rent and travel cost 900 units, the placement leaves a shortfall before food and insurance. This is hypothetical arithmetic, not a claim about any employer. Convert expenses into a common currency and check the actual payment schedule.

Check the exact job, not a general internship programme

An official internship programme page may describe its usual benefits without offering a specific vacancy today. Find the individual posting, note its role ID, duties and deadline, and confirm whether your degree, nationality, residence and work-authorisation status qualify. A remote position can still restrict the country from which the work is performed.

  1. Locate the official employer vacancy and record its current recruitment status.
  2. Check paid hours, stipend or wage conditions in the specific notice.
  3. Calculate travel, rent, insurance, visa costs and any amount payable before the first stipend.
  4. Check academic enrolment, graduation timing and local permission to work.
  5. Tailor your CV with verifiable course projects, work or volunteering rather than invented experience.

See placement planning, UK graduate requirements and the individual vacancy directory. Only the named provider can confirm current recruitment and payment terms.
