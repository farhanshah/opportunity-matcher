Remote does not automatically mean international

An internship can be advertised as “remote” but still require the intern to live in the provider's country, work specific local hours or hold permission to work there. The employer may have payroll, tax, security or equipment restrictions. Compare the eligibility paragraph on the individual official vacancy with your actual country of residence; the employer's website location alone does not answer the question.

Listing wordingWhat to ask
Remote within one countryWhich residence and work-authorisation rules apply to applicants?
Fully remote or worldwideAre there excluded countries, payroll limitations or time-zone expectations?
Unpaid remote placementIs it lawful for the location and compatible with the student's course requirements?
Hybrid internshipHow many on-site days are required, and who pays travel or relocation costs?

Illustrative example: A student in one country sees a remote marketing internship at a company based abroad. Before applying, they should check where the work must be performed, whether university enrolment is required, the supervisor's working hours and whether a stipend can be paid to an overseas account. No part of the word “remote” establishes those details.

Evidence that makes a remote internship useful

Check whether the position has defined work, an identified supervisor and realistic deliverables. A project-based role might require research notes, a prototype, an analytical report or regular meetings. Ask how feedback is given, whether remote equipment is provided and how confidential data is handled. Do not upload identity documents to an unverified recruiting form.

  1. Find the provider's individual requisition and check the current application status.
  2. Copy any residency, citizenship, time-zone or work-permission restriction from the official posting.
  3. Match the job's expected deliverables to work you've actually completed.
  4. Check the duration, pay or expense treatment and expected working hours.
  5. Apply through the official system and record the confirmation.

For preparation, read the graduate eligibility guide, the student CV guide and warning signs of misleading recruitment. Current individual roles can be researched in the verified internship directory; general programme pages should not be treated as confirmed openings.

How to check eligibility

Remote does not necessarily mean unrestricted international employment. Verify residence, payroll jurisdiction, time zone and supervision. These resources compare the route without asserting that every linked scheme is open. Follow the individual organiser's current announcements.

  1. Check the official source and exact programme identity.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualification and location restrictions.
  3. Verify admissions, nominations and document requirements.
  4. Record the final provider deadline and funding terms.
