How to check eligibility

Remote does not necessarily mean unrestricted international employment. Verify residence, payroll jurisdiction, time zone and supervision. These resources compare the route without asserting that every linked scheme is open. Follow the individual organiser's current announcements.

  1. Check the official source and exact programme identity.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualification and location restrictions.
  3. Verify admissions, nominations and document requirements.
  4. Record the final provider deadline and funding terms.
