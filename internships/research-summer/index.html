Technical and research summer study programmes

Research placements, summer courses and lab internships are different

A summer research internship is an opportunity to work on a defined project with supervision. A summer school can instead be a taught course; a departmental laboratory assistant job may be paid employment. Make sure you know which arrangement the official programme offers before writing an application or estimating costs.

FormatWhat to verifyWhat to show in your application
Supervised research internshipProject, eligible subjects, supervisor, duration and visa or employment rulesRelevant techniques, coursework and real project evidence
Summer student programmeStudent enrolment, study year, nationality and placement methodTranscript and motivation for the named research area
Taught scientific summer schoolCourse schedule, credits or certificates, tuition and accommodationAcademic background and reasons to attend the course

Worked example: A student who completed a laboratory project using chromatography can identify the specific sample-preparation and analysis methods used and one error source they had to control. That is stronger evidence than “passionate about science.” The applicant should not claim instruments, publications or experiments they did not actually use.

Check the terms before selecting a programme

A programme may require enrolment in a particular degree year throughout the placement. Another may accept recent graduates but require a work permit for the host country. Some institutions pay a stipend while participants pay flights and housing. Review each cost against the programme's current official announcement.

  1. Confirm the organiser's official website and the exact summer intake.
  2. Check degree enrolment, required subject, eligible countries and languages.
  3. Compare project topics with methods you actually know or can learn.
  4. Record dates, accommodation, stipend, travel costs and work authorisation.
  5. Follow the provider's portal and keep the submission confirmation.

For concrete preparation use the CERN programme research, internship CV guide and application evidence checklist. A standing research programme is not proof of a current vacancy.
