ADB Internship Programme

Development-bank internships in relevant study fields

What the ADB Internship Program actually requires

The Asian Development Bank's official Internship Program page describes research-oriented placements, generally lasting eight to twelve weeks. It is designed for students studying at graduate level, not for every recent graduate or undergraduate. The provider also distinguishes its long-standing internship scheme from the individual assignment notices that applicants can actually apply for.

ADB's published 2026 application round used a 16 March 2026 deadline, which has passed. The official programme page consulted for this guide lists no current internship assignments. Neither the old deadline nor the programme's existence establishes an open 2027 vacancy. Check current ADB opportunities before preparing a role-specific application.

Pay and costs: compare the actual placement terms

ADB says interns usually receive a stipend, with exceptions for certain special school arrangements. The programme guidance also notes that applicants ordinarily cover accommodation, food, local transport and part of the airfare. Comprehensive insurance is required for the placement. Its partial air travel support is not the same as a fully funded international move.

Practical example: A graduate student planning a placement away from home should record the stipend as one item, then add rent, local travel, meals, insurance and remaining airfare. The relevant question is whether the total costs are affordable, not simply whether the advertisement uses the word “paid.” The exact amount and location should be checked against the specific assignment rather than assumed from general programme material.

How selection and application work

  1. Confirm your nationality and degree enrolment meet the official programme-level rules.
  2. Open the “Current Opportunities” section and find a specific internship assignment. If none is listed, don't manufacture a vacancy from the general internship page.
  3. Read the assignment's department, tasks, location and final deadline.
  4. Prepare a factual CV and proof of enrolment, plus any further materials specified by the assignment.
  5. Apply through ACES and monitor the official application status. An interview or selection is not guaranteed.

ADB's published FAQ also states that an internship does not automatically lead to a staff job. This matters when comparing a short-term research placement with a graduate employment contract.
