DAAD EPOS Scholarships: Development-Related Courses

Programme research and source checks, updated 11 November 2026

Programme research only. This page is not an individually verified open vacancy. Follow the provider's latest application notice before treating a date or funding offer as current.

Who the programme is for

DAAD's Development-Related Postgraduate Courses programme supports eligible graduates from developing countries to study selected development-related postgraduate courses at German institutions. The award is linked to specific courses and candidates' development-related professional preparation.

Latest official-source information

The DAAD guidance says applications are sent directly to the relevant postgraduate course, and the university selection process then recommends candidates for a DAAD scholarship. The date is set by the individual eligible course rather than a single application deadline for every applicant.

Organiser: DAAD. Read current official instructions ↗

How to prepare and avoid eligibility mistakes

Start with the DAAD course list and check the applicable programme, country of residence, first degree, work experience and language criteria. If your course shortlist is not eligible for EPOS, do not assume that a general DAAD award can be used instead. Prepare accurate professional and academic evidence for the current application documents.

  1. Read the newest official announcement and select the correct programme pathway.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualifications, experience and any nominated institution.
  3. Check the genuine submission window, documents, financial support and exclusions.
  4. Submit directly using the provider's named application system.
