Check the application route

Before preparing an application, confirm citizenship, residence, study level or employment stage, and the correct route for the current cycle. A government scholarship may require a country nomination or university admission; an internship needs a specific job notice and work-location details; a research fellowship may require a host.

  1. Open the current provider programme or vacancy notice.
  2. Check every mandatory eligibility rule and actual funding component.
  3. Record submission, institutional nomination and deadline requirements.
  4. Use the official portal and keep submission confirmation.
