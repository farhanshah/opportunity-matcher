Erasmus+ Youth Exchanges, Traineeships and Mobility

Programme research and source checks, updated 11 November 2026

Programme research only. This page is not an individually verified open vacancy. Follow the provider's latest application notice before treating a date or funding offer as current.

Who the programme is for

Erasmus+ supports several different kinds of mobility. Students can explore traineeships, university exchanges and Erasmus Mundus joint master's degrees, while youth organisations coordinate many short exchange projects. These are not all individual scholarship applications.

Latest official-source information

The European Commission explains that youth exchanges are usually organised by participating groups and that an organisation applies for grant support. The eligible participant age range for youth exchanges is generally 13 to 30, with other requirements applying to group leaders. For a degree or traineeship, the student's institution and specific scheme matter.

Organiser: European Commission Erasmus+. Read current official instructions ↗

How to prepare and avoid eligibility mistakes

Check whether you must apply through a university, a youth organisation or a programme consortium. Review your country of residence, institution participation, destination and the exact support. Distinguish Erasmus+ exchange routes from the separate Erasmus Mundus master's guide so you don't send a single application to the wrong system.

  1. Read the newest official announcement and select the correct programme pathway.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualifications, experience and any nominated institution.
  3. Check the genuine submission window, documents, financial support and exclusions.
  4. Submit directly using the provider's named application system.
