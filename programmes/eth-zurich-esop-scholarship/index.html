ETH Zurich ESOP Scholarship 2027: Masters Eligibility

Official-provider research reviewed 11 November 2026

This is programme research, not an automatically verified open vacancy. Current application eligibility and closing times must be confirmed on the provider's latest official page.

What this programme offers and who it is for

ETH Zurich's Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme is intended for excellent applicants entering eligible master's programmes. It is tied to academic admission and uses the university's own electronic application process. The programme is not a general scholarship that can be transferred to any institution in Switzerland.

Official application status and route

The ETH Zurich official page identifies 1 to 30 November 2026 as the application window for master's study beginning in autumn 2027. The university states that ESOP applications belong in the first master's application window. Readers should verify the current dates and exact time zone directly because this fixed-cycle information ages quickly.

Prepare accurate application evidence

Check the required academic standard, master's course choice, research pre-proposal and references before starting. Applicants already enrolled in an ETH master's degree or already holding a master's degree may face restrictions under ESOP. Use the provider's instructions, not general Swiss scholarship pages, to make the final decision.

  1. Check the exact provider programme and current application cycle.
  2. Confirm citizenship, degree or experience requirements and any institutional approval.
  3. Record the real funding, costs and application closing time.
  4. Use the official recruitment or admission route, and retain the confirmation.
