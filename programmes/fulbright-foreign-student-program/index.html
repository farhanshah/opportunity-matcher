Fulbright Foreign Student Program 2027: Country Applications

Official-provider research reviewed 11 November 2026

This is programme research, not an automatically verified open vacancy. Current application eligibility and closing times must be confirmed on the provider's latest official page.

What this programme offers and who it is for

The Fulbright Foreign Student Program supports eligible graduate students, researchers and young professionals from participating countries in study or research in the United States. It is not one centrally administered application for all applicants: binational Fulbright commissions and U.S. embassies manage selection and requirements by country.

Official application status and route

Country eligibility is the first gate. The official U.S. State Department guidance explains that citizenship requirements, available awards and deadlines vary by participating location. You must check the Fulbright commission or embassy responsible for your country, not copy a closing date from another country.

Prepare accurate application evidence

University admission may be managed through different placement arrangements. Some selected applicants use programme-supported placement, while others are responsible for admissions to a particular U.S. institution. Confirm your country's approach before preparing test results, statements and reference documents.

  1. Check the exact provider programme and current application cycle.
  2. Confirm citizenship, degree or experience requirements and any institutional approval.
  3. Record the real funding, costs and application closing time.
  4. Use the official recruitment or admission route, and retain the confirmation.
