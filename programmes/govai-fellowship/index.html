GovAI Summer Fellowship 2027: AI Governance Research

Official-provider research reviewed 11 November 2026

This is programme research, not an automatically verified open vacancy. Current application eligibility and closing times must be confirmed on the provider's latest official page.

What this programme offers and who it is for

GovAI's 2027 UK Summer Fellowship Research Track is a three-month London-based programme for candidates interested in AI governance research and policy analysis. The organiser describes a supervised research project, seminars and opportunities to develop research skills. This differs from a university degree scholarship and from a general software engineering internship.

Official application status and route

The official 2027 Research Track page gives a June to August 2027 programme period and an application deadline of 22 November 2026 at 23:59 UK time. These are provider-published cycle facts checked on 11 November 2026, not a promise that the application remains open at a future visit.
