Humboldt Research Fellowship: Eligibility and Calls

Programme research and source checks, updated 11 November 2026

Programme research only. This page is not an individually verified open vacancy. Follow the provider's latest application notice before treating a date or funding offer as current.

Who the programme is for

The Humboldt Research Fellowship supports postdoctoral and experienced researchers conducting research in Germany with a suitable academic host. The foundation describes it as a research stay for eligible researchers from outside Germany rather than a general taught-master's scholarship.

Latest official-source information

At the 11 November 2026 source check, the foundation said that the maximum applications for the current call had been received and that a new call was scheduled to open on 15 November 2026. This future date is not a guarantee that the new portal will be open at the time you visit.

Organiser: Alexander von Humboldt Foundation. Read current official instructions ↗

How to prepare and avoid eligibility mistakes

An application should fit the applicant's postdoctoral stage and research plan. Consult the current host, degree timing and research criteria, then secure a suitable researcher to support the project. Check which research and personal documents, references and invitation evidence the foundation requires in the new call.

  1. Read the newest official announcement and select the correct programme pathway.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualifications, experience and any nominated institution.
  3. Check the genuine submission window, documents, financial support and exclusions.
  4. Submit directly using the provider's named application system.
