Choose your application route, not just a famous programme

The programmes listed here have different selection systems. A scholarship application can require country-specific selection, a university nomination, or a separate course admission. A research fellowship may require the host institution to apply with the candidate, while an internship requires an individual recruitment record. Treat these differences as the first eligibility check.
