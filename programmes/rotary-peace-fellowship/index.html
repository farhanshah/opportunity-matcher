Rotary Peace Fellowship: 2027–28 and Next Cycle

Programme research and source checks, updated 11 November 2026

Programme research only. This page is not an individually verified open vacancy. Follow the provider's latest application notice before treating a date or funding offer as current.

Who the programme is for

Rotary's peace centres programme supports selected candidates pursuing a master's degree or a professional development certificate in peace and development. These routes are different from ordinary postgraduate research fellowships. Relevant professional experience, evidence of leadership and the course pathway matter in deciding which route fits.

Latest official-source information

The official Rotary page states that applications for the 2027–28 intake have closed and that the next 2028–29 application will be available from February 2027. This is a research page, not an open-application announcement.

Organiser: Rotary International. Read current official instructions ↗

How to prepare and avoid eligibility mistakes

Before applying, compare the master's route with the certificate route. Read Rotary's current rules on work experience, academic background, programme centres, English evidence and any restrictions on Rotary members or family. Record the appropriate centre and complete the official form in the next published cycle.

  1. Read the newest official announcement and select the correct programme pathway.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualifications, experience and any nominated institution.
  3. Check the genuine submission window, documents, financial support and exclusions.
  4. Submit directly using the provider's named application system.
