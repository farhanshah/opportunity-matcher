How Stipendium Hungaricum works

Stipendium Hungaricum is the Hungarian Government's scholarship for international students, run by the Tempus Public Foundation. It is based on agreements between Hungary and partner countries (Sending Partners), so your nationality decides whether you can apply, which degree levels are open to you and which subjects you can choose.

Two applications, one deadline

You apply online on the Tempus Public Foundation platform, which usually opens in mid-November, and you must also meet your Sending Partner's requirements by the same mid-January deadline (15 January for the 2026/27 intake). The Sending Partner then nominates candidates, universities run their own admission tests or interviews, and results arrive around June and July.

Things that commonly go wrong

  • Choosing a subject or degree level that is not included in your country's agreement with Hungary.
  • Applying only on the Hungarian platform and forgetting the Sending Partner's own documents or registration.
  • Budgeting on the stipend alone: the bachelor's and master's stipend is a contribution to living costs, not enough to live on, so plan extra money for food and personal costs.

Example: A hypothetical applicant who wants a master's degree in computer science should first check that their country's Sending Partner offers master's places in that field. If it does, they apply on the Stipendium Hungaricum platform and complete the Sending Partner's process before mid-January, then prepare for the university's entrance interview.
