How the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships work

The Swiss Confederation awards these scholarships to young researchers from more than 180 countries, through the Federal Commission for Scholarships for Foreign Students. They are research scholarships: you need a master's degree for a PhD or research fellowship, and a professor at a Swiss university who agrees to supervise you before you apply.

ItemWhat the official guidance says
TypesResearch fellowship (up to 12 months), PhD scholarship (renewable in 12-month steps up to 36 months), and an art scholarship for some countries
Monthly amount (2027–28 call)CHF 2,450, which covers the living costs of one person; it is not a salary
Age (2027–28 call)Born after 31 December 1991 (maximum age 35)
SupervisorA letter from a professor at a Swiss university is mandatory
Where to applyThe Swiss embassy or the national institution named for your country; deadlines fall between September and December depending on the country

Find your supervisor first

Because the supervisor letter is mandatory, the real first step is contacting Swiss professors months before your embassy deadline with a research proposal and a timeline. Without that letter the application cannot be submitted, however strong your grades are.

Costs the scholarship does not cover

Tuition fees are reduced or waived for some programmes but not for all, and some PhD fees remain your responsibility. The scholarship also does not pay for fieldwork or conferences. Check the fee rules of the host university and budget for them.

Warning about fake offers: the Swiss authorities warn about fake "Swiss Government scholarships" advertised by email and social media. Applications are free and go only through your embassy or national institution.

Example: A hypothetical environmental engineer with a master's degree, born in 1993, wants a PhD in Switzerland. They should first confirm their country is on the list for research scholarships, then find a supervisor, then submit through the Swiss embassy in their own country before its deadline.

Check the application route

Before preparing an application, confirm citizenship, residence, study level or employment stage, and the correct route for the current cycle. A government scholarship may require a country nomination or university admission; an internship needs a specific job notice and work-location details; a research fellowship may require a host.
