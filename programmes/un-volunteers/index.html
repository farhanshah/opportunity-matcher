UN Volunteers: Field and Online Opportunities

Programme research and source checks, updated 11 November 2026

Programme research only. This page is not an individually verified open vacancy. Follow the provider's latest application notice before treating a date or funding offer as current.

Who the programme is for

The UN Volunteers programme connects individuals with volunteer assignments in UN entities and partner organisations. UNV's Unified Volunteering Platform covers different assignment types. The conditions of field assignments and online volunteering should never be conflated.

Latest official-source information

The UNV website states that online volunteer assignments are unpaid and may be carried out remotely for limited time commitments. They are not employment contracts and do not provide a living allowance. Other onsite UN Volunteer assignments have separate service conditions and candidate requirements.

Organiser: United Nations Volunteers. Read current official instructions ↗

How to prepare and avoid eligibility mistakes

Use the official platform to read each Description of Assignment, eligibility rules and language or professional requirements. Check whether a position is online or onsite and whether relocation, insurance or living support applies. Do not describe an unpaid online volunteer assignment as a fully funded internship.

  1. Read the newest official announcement and select the correct programme pathway.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualifications, experience and any nominated institution.
  3. Check the genuine submission window, documents, financial support and exclusions.
  4. Submit directly using the provider's named application system.
