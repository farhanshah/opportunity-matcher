Asia-Pacific Scholarships 2027: Japan, Korea and Beyond

Study awards across Asia and the Pacific

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

Scholarship routes in the Asia-Pacific region can be linked to diplomatic missions, national scholarship agencies or an individual university. The correct route is important: an embassy nomination and a university nomination can use different timetables.

Japan's MEXT and South Korea's GKS provide useful examples of why study level, route and applicant country must be checked separately. Other universities may use their own research admissions and financial support requirements.

Compare the language of instruction, degree recognition, arrival dates, insurance and any prerequisite course. Never assume an award in one country applies to another simply because a directory groups them together.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
