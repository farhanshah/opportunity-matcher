Australia Awards, RTP and university scholarships compared

Fully funded study in Australia for international students comes mainly from two government schemes with very different purposes. Australia Awards are development scholarships for citizens of participating countries, who must go home afterwards. Research Training Program (RTP) scholarships are research scholarships awarded by universities to domestic and international students doing a research master's or PhD.

Australia Awards: check your country profile first

If your country is not a participating country, you cannot apply. Eligibility, priority fields of study and required documents are set in your country's profile, and the Australia Awards Scholarships Policy Handbook sets the general rules. The awards are not available to Australian permanent residents or intending migrants, and scholars who do not leave Australia for two years after finishing can be charged the full cost of the scholarship. Applications for each intake are usually taken in your home country during the first half of the previous year.

RTP: stipends differ by university

The Australian Government funds universities through the RTP, and universities decide who receives the tuition offset and stipend. Research master's students can be supported for up to two years full-time. Base and maximum stipend rates are announced by 1 October each year, but universities set their own amounts within those rules. For example, published 2026 full-time rates included $35,656 a year at Griffith University, $37,010 at QUT, $37,145 at Monash and $42,754 at the University of Sydney, so compare the exact university before you apply.

Example: A hypothetical agricultural scientist from a participating country who wants a coursework master's and plans to return home is a natural fit for Australia Awards. A graduate who wants a PhD and might stay in research in Australia should instead approach supervisors and apply for an RTP scholarship at each university.

Official sources: DFAT Australia Awards Scholarships and the Department of Education Research Training Program.

How to check eligibility

Australia Awards can restrict eligible countries, study fields and return conditions. Compare those rules against any university-specific funding. For Australia, decide first whether you need a development award (Australia Awards) or a research award (RTP), because the application goes to different organisations.

  1. Check the official source and exact programme identity.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualification and location restrictions.
  3. Verify admissions, nominations and document requirements.
  4. Record the final provider deadline and funding terms.
