How to find the right programme

Canadian funding may come from university entrance awards, supervisor-supported graduate placements and a small number of large national or institutional competitions. Confirm whether an advertised amount is a one-time credit, an annual stipend, or a total award.

International undergraduate candidates should check admission, scholarships and demonstrated financial need as separate processes. Awards from an institution may be restricted to a named campus, programme or degree level.

Research applicants should contact suitable departments and compare tuition, research duties, housing and healthcare. The length of an award matters as much as the headline amount.

How international students are funded in Canada

Canada has very few national scholarships for international students, and the biggest one changed recently. Most funding is attached to a specific university: entrance awards for undergraduates, and funding packages from the department or supervisor for graduate research students.

Vanier has ended

Many older articles still describe the Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship. Its final competition ran in fall 2024. From 2025, the federal research agencies merged their doctoral awards into the Canada Graduate Research Scholarship – Doctoral (CGRS D), and international doctoral students enrolled at an eligible Canadian institution can apply, with a share of awards reserved for them. Applications go through the university, which sets its own earlier deadline.

Undergraduate: the Pearson example

The Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship at the University of Toronto covers tuition, books, incidental fees and full residence support for four years, for about 37 students a year. You cannot apply on your own initiative: your secondary school must nominate you, and schools can nominate only one student a year. It does not cover travel, visa fees or living costs outside residence, so families still need to plan for those.

Example: A hypothetical student who wants a PhD in Canada should first secure admission with a supervisor and a funding package. Once enrolled, they can ask the graduate office about the CGRS D internal deadline, rather than searching for a Vanier call that no longer exists.

Official sources: Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship and the Vanier notice pointing to CGRS D.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
