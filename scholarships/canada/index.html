Scholarships in Canada for International Students 2027

Canadian study funding and admission

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

Canadian funding may come from university entrance awards, supervisor-supported graduate placements and a small number of large national or institutional competitions. Confirm whether an advertised amount is a one-time credit, an annual stipend, or a total award.

International undergraduate candidates should check admission, scholarships and demonstrated financial need as separate processes. Awards from an institution may be restricted to a named campus, programme or degree level.

Research applicants should contact suitable departments and compare tuition, research duties, housing and healthcare. The length of an award matters as much as the headline amount.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
