Scholarships in Europe 2027: Country and Programme Guide

Europe-wide and country-specific scholarships

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

Europe is not one scholarship system. Germany's DAAD programmes, UK national awards and European joint-degree programmes have different providers, applicant pools and application schedules. First choose a course or academic field, then identify the awarding institution.

For Erasmus Mundus joint master's degrees, admission normally follows the programme consortium. The general Erasmus+ website does not accept a single application for every degree. Country or university scholarships can impose extra language and residence conditions.

When comparing European programmes, note degree recognition, tuition charging rules, travel support and whether the award covers the entire multi-country study period. Always use the programme's latest official source.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
