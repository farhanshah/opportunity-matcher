Application guidance

Before applying

A scholarship's title does not establish who qualifies. Check the current award conditions, citizenship, residence, degree, language and university admission requirements. Confirm exactly what tuition, travel and living support is covered. Check the actual sponsor, degree stage, citizenship and residence restrictions, then record each application stage separately.

  1. Check the named provider and current programme rules.
  2. Record eligibility, admission and the deadline time zone.
  3. Compare actual costs and funding components.
