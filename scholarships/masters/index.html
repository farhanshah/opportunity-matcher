Three different ways master's scholarships are awarded

Funding for a master's degree can be awarded through the university, a national programme or the degree consortium. The route matters because the funding application may be automatic with admission, require a separate essay, or depend on a university nomination submitted on your behalf. A generic scholarship list does not tell you which route your intended course uses.

RouteWhat happens firstWhat to check
University-linked awardApply to an eligible degree, then follow university funding instructionsCourse eligibility, nomination, required research proposal and award deadline
Government-sponsored awardCheck an eligible citizenship or residence route and required professional backgroundCountry restrictions, return obligations, funded degree types and separate admissions
Joint international degreeApply to the specific consortium and its named intakeParticipating universities, mobility, tuition terms and whether scholarships are available

Example: A hypothetical applicant wants a two-year European joint master's degree. Before preparing a generic funding essay, they should identify the actual course consortium, whether the programme requires study in multiple countries, and whether the degree application also serves as the scholarship application. A scholarship at a different consortium cannot automatically fund that course.

Compare the degree cost against the real award

List tuition, rent, insurance, travel, materials and any second-location move for the duration of the degree. A fee reduction may be valuable without covering living expenses. A maintenance stipend might not cover relocation or dependants. Record the provider's exact benefits, currency and payment timing rather than treating the word “full” as sufficient.

  1. Find the official course and confirm it accepts your previous qualification.
  2. Check the eligible nationality or residence group for the named scholarship.
  3. Verify whether admission and funding are one application or two separate steps.
  4. Identify references, language evidence, academic records and any required statement.
  5. Record all course, nomination and funding deadlines separately before applying.

Compare the Erasmus Mundus application route, the Commonwealth master's programme guidance and the application-process checklist. The research guides do not establish that a named 2027 intake is open.
