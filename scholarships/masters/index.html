How to check eligibility

Many master's awards apply only to participating courses or institutions. Confirm the admission route before assuming the scholarship is suitable. These resources compare the route without asserting that every linked scheme is open. Follow the individual organiser's current announcements.

  1. Check the official source and exact programme identity.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualification and location restrictions.
  3. Verify admissions, nominations and document requirements.
  4. Record the final provider deadline and funding terms.
