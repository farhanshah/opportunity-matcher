Middle East Scholarships 2027: Eligibility and Admission

University and government award research

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

Funding in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the United Arab Emirates and Türkiye is offered through different public institutions and universities. It is important to separate a scholarship programme's existence from a currently announced intake.

Some awards may be linked to a particular degree, institution or nationality. Others are administered alongside university admissions. Check whether Arabic, Turkish or English language evidence is accepted for your intended programme.

International applicants should consider housing, travel, visa sponsorship and any post-study obligations. If a programme page lacks current dates, use its official admissions contact to confirm the next round rather than relying on an aggregator's deadline.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
