The main UK scholarships for international students, compared

There is no single UK scholarship form. The large government schemes each have their own eligibility, and most university scholarships only open once you hold an offer. Matching your country and situation to the right scheme saves a wasted application.

ScholarshipWho can applyCoversHow you apply
CheveningCitizens of Chevening-eligible countries with at least two years' (2,800 hours) work experience after their first degreeFull one-year master's: tuition, living allowance, travelDirectly to Chevening; choose three UK master's courses and hold an unconditional offer by the deadline

Chevening: the work-experience rule

Chevening requires 2,800 hours of work experience gained after your undergraduate degree, which is about two years full-time. Part-time and voluntary work count. Chevening has said this rule is the most common reason applications are ineligible, so calculate your hours before writing essays. You must also commit to returning to your home country for at least two years afterwards.

Commonwealth: the nominating agency decides first

Commonwealth Master's Scholarships start with a nominating agency in your country, which runs its own selection and may set an earlier deadline and extra rules. The CSC will not normally fund a second UK master's degree without justification.

GREAT: country-specific places at named universities

GREAT Scholarships are jointly funded by the British Council, the GREAT Britain campaign and participating universities. Each university offers places for specific countries only, so check the university list for your country.

Example: A hypothetical Pakistani graduate with three years of work experience could apply for Chevening and, separately, through Pakistan's nominating agency for a Commonwealth Master's Scholarship. If neither succeeds, a GREAT Scholarship at a university offering places for Pakistan would cover part of the tuition only, so they would need other funding for living costs.

Official sources: Chevening eligibility, Commonwealth Scholarship Commission and GREAT Scholarships (British Council).

How to check eligibility

UK scholarships may require a university course application, a scholarship form and sometimes a country nomination. Check each current cycle rather than assuming one general UK deadline. For Chevening, Commonwealth and GREAT, confirm your citizenship is on the current eligible list before preparing anything else, because each scheme covers different countries.

  1. Check the official source and exact programme identity.
  2. Confirm citizenship, qualification and location restrictions.
  3. Verify admissions, nominations and document requirements.
  4. Record the final provider deadline and funding terms.
