First-degree funding follows different routes

Undergraduate awards may be attached to university admission, administered by a national scholarship agency or offered through a school nomination. A study destination does not tell you which path applies to your passport or school-leaving qualifications. Confirm that the award accepts applicants seeking their first bachelor's degree and does not require prior university enrolment.

Application pathwayFirst questionEvidence you may need
University merit scholarshipAre incoming international first-year students eligible and is award consideration automatic?School transcript, admission application and any required test results
Government scholarshipWhich nationalities, sending partners or embassy routes qualify?Citizenship, school credentials and country-specific instructions
Need-based supportDoes the institution evaluate family finances and accept international applicants?Provider-specified financial information, not invented income figures
Departmental or specialist awardAre there eligible subjects, portfolios or entrance requirements?Course selection and relevant academic or creative work

Example: A student finishing secondary school may need to apply for university admission in October but submit a scholarship form in December. A separate embassy award may require an earlier nomination. Record all three steps independently instead of copying one deadline to every application.

Check qualification and funding details

School certificates are not recognised identically in every country. Before paying an application fee, confirm whether the university accepts your qualification and whether a foundation year or specific subject level is required. If the provider offers an English-language waiver, check the exact permitted evidence and whether the scholarship has its own requirement.

  1. Check the intended degree, official admissions route and current cycle.
  2. Verify nationality, residence, first-degree and school qualification rules.
  3. Separate tuition support from accommodation, health coverage, travel and living expenses.
  4. Record reference, admission, nomination and funding deadlines.
  5. Use genuine coursework and activities in essays; avoid claiming achievements you cannot substantiate.

Read the eligibility checklist, document guide and Stipendium Hungaricum programme research for examples of different routes. An official programme page does not prove its next application round is open.
