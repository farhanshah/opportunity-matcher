Scholarships in the USA for International Students 2027

USA scholarships and university funding

Research guide, not a live application listing. Check the specific provider before relying on funding, nationality eligibility or any deadline.

How to find the right programme

Study funding in the United States is spread across academic departments, external foundations, graduate assistantships and country-specific government awards. There is no single national form covering all universities. Check whether the award is linked to an admission application or separate sponsor competition.

For graduate-level opportunities, look at faculty funding, assistantship eligibility and the applicant's existing qualification. Some well-known awards are open only to certain citizenships or study stages. A full tuition waiver can still leave required insurance or living costs.

For undergraduate study, compare institutional aid and admission requirements before selecting a university. A reference to 'fully funded' is not a substitute for the official costs table and conditions for international students.

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the latest official programme or individual vacancy announcement.
  2. Check applicant citizenship, residence, course or career stage and nomination requirements.
  3. Record exact deadlines, documents, conditions and funding from the current source.
