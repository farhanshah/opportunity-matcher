How to check the details without guessing

Deadlines and time zones

Record the exact closing date, clock time and time zone from the official notice. A date in your local time may differ from UTC or the provider's location. Confirm whether admission and funding applications use different deadlines.

Academic and country eligibility

Check nationality and residence separately. Some programmes require graduation before starting; others require a degree completed by the application deadline. If a provider doesn't publish a specific condition, do not assume it is unrestricted.

Funding versus actual cost

Write down what the award explicitly covers: fees, stipend, travel, insurance and visa costs. Deduct uncovered expenses from the expected support to understand whether the award is affordable for you. Do not treat a tuition-fee waiver as a living-cost stipend.

Documents and official submission

Check whether the provider wants an academic CV, transcript, degree certificate, recommendation letters, language evidence, a statement or work samples. Confirm file formats, word limits and whether references are submitted at application or interview stage.
